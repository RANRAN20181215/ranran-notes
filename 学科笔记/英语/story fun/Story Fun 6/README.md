# 📘 Story Fun 6 课程笔记

> 剑桥少儿英语备考教材 | 对应 **A2 Flyers（冲刺）**
> 适合五年级学生 | 本册为 Flyers 考试 **综合复习 + 全真模拟**

---

## 📚 单元总览

| 单元 | 故事标题 | 主题 | 核心词汇 | 核心句型 |
|:---:|----------|:----:|:---------|:---------|
| 1 | 🦢 **Swan Island** | 岛屿探索 & 自然保护 & 团队合作 | geography, birds, teamwork | `The island was covered with...`, `It had been... for years` |
| 2 | 🚲 **Brilliant Bikes** | 自行车 & 发明 & 环保出行 | transport, inventions, environment | `Bicycles were invented...`, `Riding a bike is healthier than...` |
| 3 | 🛒 **Frank's Funny Shopping Trip** | 购物 & 幽默 & 意外 | shopping, food, funny situations | `While he was paying, he realised...`, `He had never seen such...` |
| 4 | 🎂 **A Cake for a Queen** | 烘焙 & 比赛 & 创意 | cooking, competition, creativity | `The cake was decorated with...`, `If she won, she would...` |
| 5 | 🎵 **Katy's Favourite Song** | 音乐 & 梦想 & 表演 | music, instruments, performance | `She asked if she could...`, `He told her that...` |
| 6 | 👻 **William's Strangest Story** | 神秘 & 想象力 & 写作 | mystery, storytelling, emotions | `The strange thing was that...`, `Nobody knew who had...` |
| 7 | ⏳ **The Past and the Future** | 时间旅行 & 历史对比 | time, history, technology | `In the past, people used to...`, `By 2050, we will have...` |
| 8 | 🔬 **The School of Science** | 科学实验 & 好奇心 | science, experiments, discovery | `If you mix X with Y, it...`, `Have you ever wondered why...?` |

---

## 🎯 学习目标

- **综合巩固** Flyers 阶段全部核心语法与词汇
- 熟练运用 **所有时态**：一般现在/过去/将来、现在完成时、过去进行时、过去完成时
- 掌握 **被动语态** 多种时态（is done / was done / has been done / will be done）
- 熟练使用 **条件句**（First & Second Conditional）
- 掌握 **间接引语**（陈述句、疑问句、祈使句）
- 运用 **连接词** 组织篇章（however, although, therefore, meanwhile）
- 为剑桥少儿英语 **Flyers** 考试做 **完整冲刺备考**
