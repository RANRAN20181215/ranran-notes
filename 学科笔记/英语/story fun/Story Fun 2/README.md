# 📘 Story Fun 2 课程笔记

> 剑桥少儿英语备考教材 | 对应 Pre A1 Starters
> 适合二年级 ~ 三年级学生

---

## 📚 单元总览

| 单元 | 故事标题 | 主题 | 核心词汇 | 核心句型 |
|:---:|----------|:----:|:---------|:---------|
| 1 | 🏠 **Our Funny Home** | 房间 & 方位 & 数字 | rooms, furniture, numbers 11-20 | `There is/are...`, `...is in/on/under...` |
| 2 | 🐙 **Jill's Jellyfish** | 动物 & 猜测游戏 | animals, body parts, colours | `How many legs?`, `What colour?`, Wh- questions |
| 3 | 🎸 **Uncle Fred and Me** | 爱好 & 天气 & 服装 | hobbies, weather, clothes | `I like / He likes...`, `He is wearing...` |
| 4 | 🌻 **Mrs Day's Garden** | 农场 & 蔬菜 & 进行时 | farm animals, vegetables | `The cow is eating.`, `Go away!` |
| 5 | 🏫 **Classmates** | 学校 & 怪兽 & 进行时 | school objects, sports, actions | `He isn't...`, `Can you see...?` |
| 6 | 🎮 **I Want That Game!** | 家庭 & 交通 & 情绪 | family, transport, toys | `I want...`, `Don't worry!` |
| 7 | 🏖️ **Monkey Beach** | 海滩 & 水果 & 活动 | beach, fruit, activities | `We're having fun!`, `Let's go...` |
| 8 | 🏆 **The Winners** | 运动 & 能力 & 团队 | basketball, sports actions | `I can / can't...`, `Can you...?` |

---

## 🎯 学习目标

- 掌握 **250+** 个进阶英语词汇（房间、动物、蔬菜、运动、交通等）
- 学会 **8组** 新句型（现在进行时、can/can't、Wh- 疑问句、物主代词等）
- 巩固现在进行时（is/are doing）的肯定、否定和疑问形式
- 熟练运用 can/can't、like/likes、want 等日常表达
- 为剑桥少儿英语 Starters 考试做充分准备
