## Unit 1 — Our New School

### Vocabulary（核心词汇）
| 词 | 中文 |
|----|------|
| desk | 书桌 |

| book | 书 |
| crayon | 蜡笔 |

| door | 门 |
| window | 窗户 |
| teacher | 老师 |
| board | 黑板 |

### Grammar 语法

**1. Where's / Where are...?（在哪里）**
```
Where's the book? — It's on the desk.
Where are the crayons? — They're in the bag.
```

**2. 方位介词**
| 介词 | 意思 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| in | 在……里面 | in the bag |
| on | 在……上面 | on the desk |
| under | 在……下面 | under the chair |
| next to | 在……旁边 | next to the door |

**3. this / these（这个/这些）**
```
What's this? — It's a window.（单数）
What are these? — They're windows.（复数）
```

### Phonics
| 音素 | 代表词 |
|------|--------|
| /p/ | pencil, pink, purple |
| /b/ | book, bag, board |

### Cross-curricular（跨学科）
- Be kind at school — 在学校要友善

### Literature（文学）
- 剧本：*The first day* — 社交情感技能：帮助他人（Helping others）

### Exam Prep（考级对标 — Pre A1 Starters）
- Reading & Writing Part 1：看图选词
- Speaking Parts 1–2：指物说词、简单问答

### 建议学习步骤
1. 听音频跟读词汇 ×3 遍
2. 用实物/图片玩 Where's the...? 游戏
3. 练写 this/these 句型
4. 做 phonics 拼读（p/b 对比）
5. 朗读剧本 + 角色扮演

## Unit 2 — All About Us

### Vocabulary
| 类别 | 词汇 |
|------|------|
| 家庭 | mother, father, brother, sister, baby, grandma, grandpa |
| 身体 | hair, eyes, ears, nose, mouth, hands, feet, face, head |
| 形容词 | brown, blue, green, long, short, big, small |

### Grammar

**1. is / are（be 动词）**
```
She is Jenny.（单数）
They are my friends.（复数）
```

**2. Who is she / he?（谁）**
```
Who is she? — She's Jenny.
Who is he? — He's Jim.
```

**3. have / haven't got（有/没有）**
```
I've got brown hair.
They haven't got green eyes.
Have you got red hair? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
```

### 重点对比
- *He's got* = He has got（缩写）
- *She's got* = She has got
- *I've got* = I have got

### Phonics
| 音素 | 代表词 |
|------|--------|
| /θ/ /ð/ | **th** — mother, brother, this, that |
| /t/ | **t** — ten, two, teacher |

### Cross-curricular
- Using our senses — 五感（see, hear, smell, taste, touch）

### Literature
- 生活故事：*Sara's favourite game* — SE：关心他人（Showing concern）

### Exam Prep
- R&W Part 2：看句子选图
- Speaking Part 2：看图片描述人物

### 学习提示
- 冉冉听说强，have/haven't got 对她来说简单，重点练**书写**
- 用家庭照片练习：*Who is she? — She's 妈妈. She's got long hair.*

| 饮品 | water, milk, juice |

### Grammar

```

```
Can I have some chocolate, please? — Here you are.
Would you like some ice cream? — Yes, please. / No, thank you.

## Unit 7 — Let's Play

### Vocabulary
| 词 | 中文 |
|----|------|
| ride a horse | 骑马 |
| swim | 游泳 |
| clean the car | 洗车 |
| play tennis | 打网球 |
| play football | 踢足球 |
| kick a ball | 踢球 |
| run | 跑步 |
| play basketball | 打篮球 |
| ride a bike | 骑自行车 |
| fly a kite | 放风筝 |

### Grammar

**1. 现在进行时（Present Continuous）**
```
What are you doing? — I'm riding a horse.
What's she doing? — She's swimming.
Are they cleaning the car? — Yes, they are. / No, they're not.
```
⚠️ 冉冉关键注意：
- I'm = I am
- He's/She's = He is/She is（此处的 's 不是 has 是 is）
- 动词 + -ing
  - swim → swimming（双写 m）
  - run → running（双写 n）
  - ride → riding（去 e + ing）

**2. can 表许可**
```
Can we play tennis? — Yes, you can, but you can't play here.
```

### Phonics
| 音素 | 代表词 |
|------|--------|
| /s/ | s — swim, sun, six, seven |

### Cross-curricular
- 照顾身体（Look after your body!）：运动与健康习惯

### Exam Prep
- Listening Part 1：听录音选图
- Speaking Part 3：看图片描述动作

### 每周节奏（按冉冉一年级课业量）
| 日 | 内容 | 时长 |
|---|------|------|
| 周一 | 新词汇 + 跟读音频 | 15min |

| 周五 | Phonics + 跨学科阅读 | 15min |
| 周末 | Literature 朗读 + Mission 项目 | 20–30min |

### 每本单元周期
- 一个单元 ≈ **2 周**（含练习册）
- 全书 9 单元 + 3 复习 ≈ **24 周 ≈ 6 个月**

### 配套资源
- 官方 Pupil's Book + Activity Book
- Power Up 官网资源（歌曲、视频、游戏）
- 闪卡 App（Quizlet / Anki）
- Pre A1 Starters 真题（考前 1 个月刷）

### 冉冉特殊关注点
- 听说强 → **重点补读写**：每单元抄写词汇、做拼写练习
- 写句子时注意大小写和标点
- 动词 -ing 双写规则需要反复练
- 物主代词 my/your/his/her 要写对
- 单数/复数（a book / books）要分清
