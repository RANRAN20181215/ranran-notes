# 📘 Unit 8 The Young Artist

> Big Question 4: **Why do we make art?**
> 主题领域: Art（艺术）| 阅读策略: Sequence（顺序）
> 文体: **Fiction（故事）**

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 故事讲一个孩子发现自己艺术天赋的过程
- 主角一开始觉得画画很难 **complex**
- 后来遇到一位艺术家，教他用不同材料创作
- 他尝试用 **clay** 做雕塑，用 **wood** 做木雕
- 他画了一幅 **three-dimensional** 的画
- 最后他的画在学校的 Art Show 展出
- 他发现艺术的真正意义是表达自己的感受

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Passive Voice — More Practice

### 不同时态的被动

| 时态 | 结构 | 例句 |
|:----|:----|:-----|
| 一般现在时 | am/is/are + v3 | The clay **is shaped** into a bowl. |
| 一般过去时 | was/were + v3 | The picture **was painted** last year. |
| 现在进行时 | am/is/are being + v3 | The show **is being prepared** now. |

### 被动句的用途

| 场景 | 例句 |
|:----|:-----|
| 强调作品本身 | This painting **is admired** by many people. |
| 不谈谁做的 | The art show **is held** every spring. |
| 描述制作过程 | First, the clay **is shaped**. Then, it **is baked**. |
| 描述材料 | This sculpture **is carved** from wood. |

### 练习 Examples

| 主动 → 被动 |
|:-----------|
| The teacher chooses the best painting. → The best painting **is chosen** by the teacher. |
| People made this statue 100 years ago. → This statue **was made** 100 years ago. |
| The artist uses bright colors. → Bright colors **are used** by the artist. |

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| Art is made by people who want to express themselves. | 艺术是由想表达自己的人创造的。 |
| The sculpture was carved from a piece of wood. | 这个雕塑是用一块木头雕刻的。 |
| The painting is admired by everyone in school. | 这幅画被学校里的每个人欣赏。 |
| What is your artwork made from? | 你的艺术品是用什么做的？ |
| I feel proud when my art is displayed. | 当我的艺术作品被展示时，我很自豪。 |
| Creativity is encouraged in art class. | 美术课鼓励创造力。 |

## 🎧 Listening & Speaking 听说练习

- 听一个年轻艺术家的故事
- 口语练习：Talking about personal artwork
  - "What did you make?"
  - "It was made from…"
  - "I was inspired by…"
  - "It is displayed in…"
- 用 Passive Voice 分享自己的作品

---

## ✍️ Writing 写作

- **Descriptive Paragraph about an Artwork**：描述自己的一件作品
  - 开头：My artwork is called "Sunset." It is painted on canvas.
  - 中间：Bright colors are used. The sun is painted in the center.
  - 结尾：This painting was made for my mom. She loved it!
- 用 4+ Passive Voice 句子

---

## 🎮 Fun Activities 趣味活动

1. **🎨 Art Show Role-play**：办一个小型画展，学生用被动语态介绍作品
2. **🔄 Passive Voice Puzzle**：把打乱的主动/被动句配对
3. **✋ Touch & Guess**：摸材料猜是什么做的（It is made from clay.）
4. **🖼️ Design Your Masterpiece**：设计和描述一件艺术品，用被动语态
