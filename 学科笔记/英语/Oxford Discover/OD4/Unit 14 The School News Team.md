# 📘 Unit 14 The School News Team

> Big Question 7: **What is the mass media?**
> 主题领域: Social Studies（社科/传媒）| 阅读策略: Sequence（顺序）
> 文体: **Fiction（故事）**

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 故事讲几个学生办学校报纸/新闻节目的经历
- 他们组成一个 **news team**（新闻团队）
- Reporter 负责采访，photographer 拍照，editor 编辑
- 他们需要找到有趣的故事来报道
- 他们采访了校长、厨师大妈、足球队长
- 最后出版了第一期学校报纸
- 故事展示新闻制作流程：选题 → 采访 → 写稿 → 编辑 → 发布

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Reported Speech — Questions & Commands

### Reported Questions 间接疑问句

| 类型 | Direct | Reported |
|:----|:-------|:---------|
| **Yes/No 问句** | "**Do** you like reading?" | She asked **if / whether** I liked reading. |
| | "**Can** you take photos?" | He asked **if** I could take photos. |
| **Wh-问句** | "**What** do you do?" | She asked **what** I did. |
| | "**Where** do you work?" | He asked **where** I worked. |

### Reported Commands 间接命令/请求

| 类型 | Direct | Reported |
|:----|:-------|:---------|
| 肯定命令 | "Write an article!" | The editor **told** me **to write** an article. |
| 否定命令 | "Don't be late!" | He **told** me **not to be** late. |
| 请求 | "Please help me." | She **asked** me **to help** her. |

### 规则总结

| 原句类型 | Reported 结构 |
|:---------|:-------------|
| 陈述句 (say/tell) | said (that) + 句子 |
| Yes/No 问句 | asked if / whether + 句子 |
| Wh-问句 | asked + wh-词 + 句子 |
| 命令/请求 | told / asked + to + 动词 |

| 英文 | 中文 |

## 🎧 Listening & Speaking 听说练习

- 听关于采访的对话
- 口语练习：Reporting an interview（报道采访内容）
  - "He said that…"
  - "She told me that…"
  - "I asked if…"
  - "They said they would…"
- 别人说一句话，你再转述给第三人

---

## ✍️ Writing 写作

- **News Article with Reported Speech**：写一篇新闻文章
  - 开头：Last week, our school news team interviewed the principal.
  - 中间：The principal said that the school would build a new library. He told us that it would be ready next year.
  - 结尾：The news team was excited to share this story.
- 用 5+ Reported Speech 句子

## 🎮 Fun Activities 趣味活动

1. **🎤 News Team Role-play**：分组组建新闻团队，制作一期学校新闻
2. **🔄 Chinese Whispers**：传话练习——原话传到最后变了多少？
3. **📰 Class Newspaper Project**：每人写一篇短文，全班出一期报纸
4. **🎭 Interview Game**：一个人扮演名人，记者采访后转述给全班
