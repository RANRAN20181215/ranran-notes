# 📘 Unit 17 Why Are Biomes Important?

> Big Question 9: **Why are biomes important?**
> 主题领域: Science（科学/生态）| 阅读策略: Compare & Contrast（比较与对比）
> 文体: **Nonfiction（信息文）**

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- **Biome**（生物群落）是一个大的自然区域，有特定的气候和生物
- 主要 biomes：
  - **Rainforest**（热带雨林）：炎热潮湿，很多动植物
  - **Desert**（沙漠）：非常干燥，白天热晚上冷
  - **Tundra**（冻原）：非常寒冷，没有树
  - **Grassland**（草原）：有很多草，适合放牧
  - **Taiga**（针叶林）：寒冷，有很多松树
  - **Ocean**（海洋）：水下 habitat
  - **Freshwater**（淡水）：河流、湖泊
- 每个 biome 都很重要——不同动物和植物生存在那里
- 读文章时 Compare & Contrast：不同 biomes 有什么不同？

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Present Perfect with For & Since

### For vs Since

| 介词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|:----|:-----|:-----|
| **for** + 时间段 | 多久 | for two years, for a long time |
| **since** + 时间点 | 从……开始 | since 2020, since last summer |

| 例句 | 说明 |
|:----|:-----|
| The Amazon rainforest **has existed for** millions of years. | 时间段 |
| It **has been** a protected area **since** 2000. | 时间点 |
| People **have lived** in the desert **for** thousands of years. | 时间段 |
| These animals **have adapted** to the cold **since** the Ice Age. | 时间点 |

### For + 时间段

| 时间段 | 例句 |
|:-------|:-----|
| **for a long time** | Polar bears have lived here **for a long time**. |
| **for 10 years** | The forest has been protected **for 10 years**. |
| **for centuries** | People have farmed this grassland **for centuries**. |
| **for months** | The desert hasn't had rain **for months**. |

### Since + 时间点

| 时间点 | 例句 |
|:-------|:-----|
| **since 2015** | This species has been endangered **since 2015**. |
| **since last year** | The rain hasn't come **since last year**. |
| **since I was young** | I have loved rainforests **since I was young**. |
| **since the beginning** | Life has existed on Earth **since the beginning**. |

### 问句 Questions

| 问句 | 回答 |
|:----|:-----|
| **How long** have you lived near the forest? | I **have lived** here **for** 5 years. |
| **How long** has the desert been dry? | It **has been** dry **since** last summer. |

---

## ✍️ Writing 写作

- **Compare & Contrast Essay about Two Biomes**：比较两种 biomes
  - 开头：Rainforests and deserts are very different biomes.
  - 中间：The rainforest has had a lot of rain for many years. The desert has been dry for a long time.
  - 结尾：Both biomes are important and need to be protected.
- 用 4+ Present Perfect with for/since
