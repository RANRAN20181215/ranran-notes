# 📘 Unit 7 Why Do We Make Art?

> Big Question 4: **Why do we make art?**
> 主题领域: Art（艺术）| 阅读策略: Identify Main Idea（找出主要观点）
> 文体: **Nonfiction（信息文）**

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 人们创造艺术有很多原因：表达感受、记录历史、美化生活
- 画家用 **canvas**（画布）和 **paintbrushes**（画笔）画画
- **Sculptor**（雕刻家）用石头或木头雕刻
- **Carpenter**（木匠）做木工，也是艺术的一种
- **Washable paint**（可水洗颜料）适合小朋友
- **Prodigy**（天才）：很小就展现惊人艺术才能
- 一幅好画有一个 **title**（标题）和 **caption**（说明文字）
- **Three-dimensional drawing**（三维画）看起来有立体感
- 读文章时找 Main Idea：人们为什么创造艺术？

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Passive Voice (Simple Present)

### 什么是 Passive Voice 被动语态？

| Active（主动） | Passive（被动） |
|:--------------|:--------------|
| The artist **paints** the picture. | The picture **is painted** by the artist. |
| 强调：谁做的 | 强调：什么被做了 |

### 结构 Formula

| 主语 | **am / is / are** | 过去分词(v3) | (by + 人) |
|:----|:-----------------|:------------|:----------|
| The picture | **is** | painted | by the artist. |
| Sculptures | **are** | made | by sculptors. |
| I | **am** | inspired | by art. |
| The clay | **is** | shaped | into a bowl. |

### 为什么用 Passive？

| 原因 | 例句 |
|:----|:-----|
| 不知道谁做的 | This painting **was made** long ago. |
| 不重要谁做的 | Washable paint **is used** for kids. |
| 强调物品本身 | The canvas **is stretched** on a frame. |

### 一般现在时被动

| 类型 | 例句 |
|:----|:-----|
| 肯定句 | Paintbrushes **are made** from hair and wood. |
| 否定句 | The picture **isn't finished** yet. |
| 疑问句 | **Is** the sculpture **made** from stone? |
| 回答 | Yes, it **is**. / No, it **isn't**. |

## 🎧 Listening & Speaking 听说练习

- 听关于艺术品的描述
- 口语练习：Describing art（描述艺术品）
  - "What is this painting called?"
  - "What is it made from?"
  - "It was painted by…"
  - "I think it's fascinating / complex / beautiful."
- 用 Passive Voice 描述艺术品

---

## ✍️ Writing 写作

- **Sentence Transformation**：把主动句改成被动句
  - Active: The artist paints the picture.
  - Passive: The picture is painted by the artist.
- 写一段话描述一幅画或雕像，用 3 个 Passive 句子
  - 开头：This statue is made from stone.
  - 中间：It was created by a sculptor…
  - 结尾：It is displayed in a museum.
