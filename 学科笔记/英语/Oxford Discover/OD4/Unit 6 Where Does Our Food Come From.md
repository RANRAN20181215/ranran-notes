# 📘 Unit 6 Where Does Our Food Come From?

> Big Question 3: **Where does our food come from?**
> 主题领域: Science（科学/食品贸易）| 阅读策略: Categorize（分类）
> 文体: **Nonfiction（信息文）**

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 很多食物不是本地生产的，而是从其他国家来的
- **Export**（出口）：一个国家把东西卖到另一个国家
- **Import**（进口）：一个国家从别国买进东西
- 例如：香蕉从热带国家 **export** 到全世界
- 苹果生长在温带，也出口到热带国家
- 有些食物很方便 **convenient**，不用煮就能吃
- 读文章时注意 Categorize：按食物类型/产地分类

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Quantifiers & Count / Non-count Nouns

### Countable Nouns 可数名词

- 可以数：one apple, two bananas, three strawberries
- 用 **a / an**：a kiwi, an apple
- 用 **many / a few / some / any**

| 用法 | 肯定句 | 否定句 | 疑问句 |
|:----|:-------|:-------|:-------|
| many | There are **many** apples. | There aren't **many** bananas. | Are there **many** kiwis? |
| a few | There are **a few** strawberries. | — | — |
| some | There are **some** bananas. | — | Would you like **some** grapes? |
| any | — | There aren't **any** apples. | Are there **any** oranges? |

### Non-count Nouns 不可数名词

- 不能数：rice, wheat, corn, butter, bread, milk
- 用 **much / a little / some / any**
- 不能加 -s

| 用法 | 肯定句 | 否定句 | 疑问句 |
|:----|:-------|:-------|:-------|
| much | — | There isn't **much** rice. | Is there **much** milk? |
| a little | There is **a little** butter. | — | — |
| some | There is **some** bread. | — | Would you like **some** corn? |
| any | — | There isn't **any** milk. | Is there **any** wheat? |

### How many vs How much

| 问句 | 回答 |
|:----|:-----|
| **How many** apples? (可数) | There are five. |
| **How much** rice? (不可数) | There is a lot. / A little. |

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| Where does our food come from? | 我们的食物从哪里来？ |
| Bananas are exported from warm countries. | 香蕉从温暖的国家出口。 |
| Many countries import rice from Asia. | 很多国家从亚洲进口大米。 |
| How many apples do we have? | 我们有多少苹果？ |
| How much rice is there? | 有多少大米？ |
| There aren't any strawberries in the fridge. | 冰箱里没有草莓了。 |
| Is there any milk? — Yes, there's a little. | 有牛奶吗？——有一点。 |
