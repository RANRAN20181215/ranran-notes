# 📘 Unit 9 What Is a City?

> Big Question 5: **What is a city?**
> 主题领域: Social Studies（社科/城市）| 阅读策略: Categorize（分类）
> 文体: **Nonfiction（信息文）**

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 城市比小镇更大、更繁忙、有更多人
- 城市有 **city hall**（市政厅）和 **city council**（市议会）管理
- **Mayor**（市长）是城市的领导者
- **Citizen**（市民）住在城市里，享有权利和 responsibilities
- **Volunteers**（志愿者）无偿帮助别人
- **Garbage collectors**（垃圾收集员）保持城市清洁
- 城市有 **canal**（运河）、**port**（港口）、**rickshaw**（人力车）
- 游客可以买 **souvenir**（纪念品）和 **antique**（古董）
- **Exotic fruits**（异国水果）在市场里卖

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Comparatives & Superlatives

### Comparatives 比较级（两者比）

| 规则 | 形容词 | 比较级 | 例句 |
|:----|:-------|:-------|:-----|
| 短词加 -er | big | **bigger** | City A is **bigger** than City B. |
| y → ier | busy | **busier** | The city is **busier** than the town. |
| 多音节用 more | exciting | **more exciting** | The city is **more exciting** than the village. |
| 不规则 | good / bad | **better / worse** | Subways are **better** than buses. |

### Superlatives 最高级（三者以上）

| 规则 | 形容词 | 最高级 | 例句 |
|:----|:-------|:-------|:-----|
| 短词加 -est | big | **the biggest** | Tokyo is **the biggest** city. |
| y → iest | busy | **the busiest** | This is **the busiest** street. |
| 多音节用 most | exciting | **the most exciting** | New York is **the most exciting** city. |
| 不规则 | good / bad | **the best / the worst** | This is **the best** museum. |

### As + Adj + As 同级比较

| 结构 | 例句 |
|:----|:-----|
| as + adj + as | The park is **as big as** the stadium. |
| not as + adj + as | The town is **not as busy as** the city. |

## 🎧 Listening & Speaking 听说练习

- 听关于城市生活的对话
- 口语练习：Comparing cities（比较城市）
  - "Which city is bigger, Beijing or Shanghai?"
  - "Is your city as busy as Tokyo?"
  - "What's the most exciting place in your city?"
- 用 Comparatives & Superlatives 比较城市

---

## ✍️ Writing 写作

- **Compare & Contrast Paragraph**：比较城市和乡村
  - 开头：The city and the countryside are very different.
  - 中间：The city is bigger and busier than the countryside. The countryside is quieter and cleaner.
  - 结尾：I think the city is more exciting, but the countryside is more relaxing.
- 用 4 个比较/最高级

---

## 🎮 Fun Activities 趣味活动

1. **🏙️ City vs Country Debate**：分组辩论城市好还是乡村好，用比较级
2. **🗺️ City Fact Quiz**：比大小（Which is bigger, Shanghai or London?）
3. **🎭 Mayor Role-play**：你是市长——描述你的城市，用最高级
4. **📊 City Ranking Chart**：给 3 个城市排名（人口/面积/好玩程度）
