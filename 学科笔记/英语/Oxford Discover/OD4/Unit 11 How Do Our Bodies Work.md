# 📘 Unit 11 How Do Our Bodies Work?

> Big Question 6: **How do our bodies work?**
> 主题领域: Science（科学/人体）| 阅读策略: Cause & Effect（因果）
> 文体: **Nonfiction（信息文）**

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 人体是一个 amazing 的机器！
- **Skeleton**（骨骼）支撑身体，保护器官
- **Muscles**（肌肉）帮助我们移动
- **Brain**（大脑）指挥所有动作
- **Heart**（心脏）把血液 pumped 到全身
- **Lungs**（肺）帮我们呼吸
- **Digestive system**（消化系统）把食物变成能量
- 读文章时找 Cause & Effect：
  - Cause（原因）：We exercise.
  - Effect（结果）：Our heart beats faster.

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Modals — Have to / Must / Should

### Have to 不得不（客观需要）

| 类型 | 例句 |
|:----|:-----|
| 肯定句 | I **have to** brush my teeth every day. |
| 否定句 | He **doesn't have to** take medicine now. |
| 疑问句 | **Do** you **have to** wear glasses? |
| 过去式 | I **had to** stay in bed yesterday. |

### Must 必须（主观/规则）

| 类型 | 例句 |
|:----|:-----|
| 肯定句 | You **must** wash your hands before eating. |
| 否定句 | You **mustn't** run in the hospital. |
| 疑问句 | **Must** I take this medicine now? |

### Should 应该（建议）

| 类型 | 例句 |
|:----|:-----|
| 肯定句 | You **should** drink more water. |
| 否定句 | You **shouldn't** eat too much candy. |
| 疑问句 | What **should** I do if I feel sick? |

### 三者的区别

| Modal | 强度 | 说明 |
|:------|:----|:-----|
| **must** | 🔴 最强 | 规则/命令：You **must** stop at a red light. |
| **have to** | 🟡 中等 | 客观需要：I **have to** wear glasses to read. |
| **should** | 🟢 建议 | 好主意：You **should** exercise every day. |

## 🎧 Listening & Speaking 听说练习

- 听关于健康和医生的建议
- 口语练习：Giving advice（给建议）
  - "What should I do if I have a headache?"
  - "You should rest and drink water."
  - "Do I have to take medicine?"
  - "Yes, you have to take it twice a day."
- 用 **have to / must / should** 给建议

## ✍️ Writing 写作

- **Health Advice Poster**：写一张健康建议海报
  - 开头：How to stay healthy
  - 中间：You have to… / You must… / You should… / You shouldn't…
  - 结尾：Follow these tips and stay healthy!
- 用 5+ Modals
