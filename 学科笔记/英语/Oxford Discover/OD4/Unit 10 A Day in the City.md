# 📘 Unit 10 A Day in the City

> Big Question 5: **What is a city?**
> 主题领域: Social Studies（社科/城市生活）| 阅读策略: Sequence（顺序）
> 文体: **Fiction（故事）**

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 故事讲一家人去城市里玩一天的经历
- 他们坐地铁到市中心
- 参观了 **museum**、**market**、**city hall**
- 在市场里看到 **souvenir**、**antique**、**exotic fruits**
- 在 **port** 看到大船和 **tuna** 捕捞
- 坐 **rickshaw** 穿过古老的街道
- 每个人都有不同的感受：城市很大、很热闹、很棒

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Comparatives & Superlatives — More Practice

### 形容词变比较级和最高级复习

| 形容词 | 比较级 | 最高级 |
|:-------|:-------|:-------|
| big | bigger | the biggest |
| small | smaller | the smallest |
| busy | busier | the busiest |
| quiet | quieter | the quietest |
| beautiful | more beautiful | the most beautiful |
| interesting | more interesting | the most interesting |
| good | better | the best |
| bad | worse | the worst |
| far | farther / further | the farthest / the furthest |

### 句型扩展

| 句型 | 例句 |
|:----|:-----|
| 两者比较 (than) | The museum is **bigger than** the library. |
| 同级比较 (as…as) | The park is **as beautiful as** the garden. |
| 否定同级 (not as…as) | The subway is **not as fast as** the taxi. |
| 最高级 (the) | This is **the busiest street** in the city. |

### 故事中的比较

| 英文 |
|:-----|
| The city is **much bigger than** our town. |
| The market is **more crowded than** the supermarket. |
| The museum is **the most interesting place** in the city. |
| The park is **as pretty as** the pictures online. |

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| The city is bigger and busier than the town. | 城市比小镇更大更繁忙。 |
| The museum is the most interesting place in the city. | 博物馆是城市里最有趣的地方。 |
| Is the city as noisy as I remember? | 这座城市和我记忆中一样吵吗？ |
| The market sells exotic fruits from different countries. | 市场卖来自不同国家的异国水果。 |
| We took a rickshaw through the old streets. | 我们坐人力车穿过老街道。 |
| The port is busier than the market. | 港口比市场更繁忙。 |

## 🎧 Listening & Speaking 听说练习

- 听在城市里问路的对话
- 口语练习：Giving directions（指路）
  - "Excuse me, where is the museum?"
  - "Go straight. Turn left at the market."
  - "It's next to the city hall."
- 口语练习：Comparing experiences（比较经历）
  - "The museum was more interesting than I thought."
  - "The food was as good as the restaurant at home."

---

## ✍️ Writing 写作

- **A Day in the City Journal**：写一篇城市一日游日记
  - 开头：Last Saturday, my family and I visited the city.
  - 中间：First, we went to the museum. It was bigger than I expected. Next, we went to the market…
  - 结尾：I had a wonderful time. The city is more exciting than our town.
- 用 4+ Comparatives / Superlatives

## 🎮 Fun Activities 趣味活动
