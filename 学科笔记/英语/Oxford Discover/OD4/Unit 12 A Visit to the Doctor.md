# 📘 Unit 12 A Visit to the Doctor

> Big Question 6: **How do our bodies work?**
> 主题领域: Science（科学/健康）| 阅读策略: Sequence（顺序）
> 文体: **Fiction（故事）**

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 故事讲一个人觉得不舒服去看医生
- 医生问他问题、做检查、给建议
- 病人：咳嗽、发烧、头疼
- 医生用 **stethoscope**（听诊器）听心跳
- 建议：多喝水，多休息，吃药
- 故事展示**看病流程**：症状 → 检查 → 诊断 → 建议

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Modals — More Practice

### 否定形式对比

| Modal | 否定 | 意思 |
|:------|:-----|:-----|
| must → **mustn't** | mustn't = 绝对不可以 | You **mustn't** play with fire. |
| have to → **don't have to** | don't have to = 没必要 | You **don't have to** wear a coat. It's warm. |
| should → **shouldn't** | shouldn't = 不应该 | You **shouldn't** eat too much sugar. |

### 问句和回答

| 问句 | 肯定回答 | 否定回答 |
|:-----|:---------|:---------|
| **Do I have to** take medicine? | Yes, you **do**. | No, you **don't**. |
| **Must** I stay in bed? | Yes, you **must**. | No, you **needn't** / **don't have to**. |
| **Should** I see a doctor? | Yes, you **should**. | No, you **shouldn't**. |

### 对话中的 Modals

| 英文 |
|:-----|
| Patient: What **should** I do, doctor? |
| Doctor: You **should** rest. You **mustn't** go outside. |
| Patient: **Do I have to** take this medicine? |
| Doctor: Yes, you **have to** take it three times a day. |
| Patient: **Should** I eat anything special? |
| Doctor: You **should** eat light food and drink warm water. |

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| How do our bodies work? | 我们的身体如何运作？ |
| I have a headache and a fever. | 我头疼还发烧。 |
| You should stay in bed and rest. | 你应该卧床休息。 |
| You have to take this medicine twice a day. | 你必须每天吃两次这个药。 |
| You mustn't eat cold food when you're sick. | 生病时你不能吃冷的食物。 |
| Do I have to go to the hospital? | 我必须去医院吗？ |

## 🎧 Listening & Speaking 听说练习

- 听看病时的对话
- 口语练习：At the doctor's（看病）
  - "What's wrong?" / "I feel sick."
  - "Where does it hurt?" / "My head hurts."
  - "What should I do?" / "You should…"
  - "Do I have to…?" / "Yes, you do."
- 角色扮演：医生和病人

---

## ✍️ Writing 写作

- **Dialogue at the Doctor's**：写一段看病对话
  - 开头：Doctor: What's the matter?
  - 中间：Patient: I have a bad cold. / Doctor: You should rest and drink warm water.
  - 结尾：Doctor: Take this medicine and you'll get better soon.
- 用 5+ Modals
