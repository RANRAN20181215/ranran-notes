# 📘 Unit 18 The Lost in the Rainforest

> Big Question 9: **Why are biomes important?**
> 主题领域: Science（科学/生态）| 阅读策略: Sequence（顺序）
> 文体: **Fiction（故事）**

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 故事讲几个孩子在热带雨林里探险
- 他们跟着一条河走，看到了各种动植物
- 雨林里有 **toucans**（犀鸟）、**monkeys**（猴子）、**butterflies**（蝴蝶）
- 高大的树形成了 **canopy**（树冠层）
- 他们发现了一个瀑布和隐藏的洞穴
- 在雨林里他们学会了 respect 自然
- 故事结尾他们明白了：biomes 是很多生物的家园，需要被保护

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Present Perfect with For & Since — More Practice

### 复习 Review

| 用法 | 例句 |
|:----|:-----|
| **for** + 时间段 | We **have walked** through the forest **for** two hours. |
| **since** + 时间点 | The monkeys **have lived** here **since** the rainforest began. |

### 肯定句、否定句、疑问句

| 类型 | 例句 |
|:----|:-----|
| 肯定句 | The toucans **have lived** in this tree **for** many years. |
| 否定句 | The river **hasn't changed** **since** last year. |
| 疑问句 | **How long have** you **been** in the rainforest? |
| 回答 | We **have been** here **for** three days. |

### 更多时态表达

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:-----|
| The rainforest **has existed for** millions of years. | 热带雨林已经存在了数百万年。 |
| These trees **have grown** here **since** the last Ice Age. | 这些树自从上一个冰河时代就长在这里。 |
| We **have seen** many animals **since** we arrived. | 我们到达以来看到了很多动物。 |
| The waterfall **has been here for** a very long time. | 这个瀑布已经存在了很长时间。 |

### 故事中的 Present Perfect

| 英文 |
|:-----|
| "How long have you lived in the rainforest?" asked Tom. |
| "I have lived here **for** 10 years," said the guide. |
| "This tree has been growing **since** before I was born!" |
| "We have walked **for** hours. Are we there yet?" |

## ✍️ Writing 写作

- **Adventure Story Paragraph**：写一段雨林冒险经历
  - 开头：Last summer, I explored a rainforest for the first time.
  - 中间：I have always wanted to see a toucan. I have walked for hours through the jungle. I have seen many amazing things since I arrived.
  - 结尾：The rainforest has existed for millions of years. We need to protect it.
- 用 4+ Present Perfect with for/since
