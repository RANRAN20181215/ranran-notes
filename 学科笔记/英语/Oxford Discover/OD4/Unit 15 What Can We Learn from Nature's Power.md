# 📘 Unit 15 What Can We Learn from Nature's Power?

> Big Question 8: **What can we learn from nature's power?**
> 主题领域: Geography / Science（地理/自然力量）| 阅读策略: Cause & Effect（因果）
> 文体: **Nonfiction（信息文）**

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 大自然的力量非常强大，有时也很危险
- **Earthquake**（地震）：地面 shaking，由地壳移动引起
- **Volcano**（火山）：岩浆从地底喷出
- **Tsunami**（海啸）：海底地震引起的巨大海浪
- **Hurricane**（飓风）：非常强的风暴
- **Tornado**（龙卷风）：旋转的风柱
- **Wildfire**（野火）：在森林或草原上燃烧的大火
- **Flood**（洪水）：水淹没陆地
- 从这些自然力量中，我们学会尊重自然、做好准备
- 读文章时找 Cause & Effect

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Past Perfect

### 什么是 Past Perfect？

- 表示**过去的过去**——先发生的动作用 Past Perfect，后发生的用 Simple Past

### 结构

| 主语 | **had** | 过去分词(v3) |
|:----|:--------|:------------|
| The volcano | **had** | erupted before the villagers left. |
| They | **had** | already left when the flood came. |

### 时间线 Timeline

```
Past Perfect     →     Simple Past     →     Now
(先发生)               (后发生)
```

- The rain **had stopped** before we went outside. → 先停雨，再出去
- The ground **had shaken** for a minute when the building fell. → 先摇了一分钟，再倒塌

### 例句 Examples

| 英文 | 说明 |
|:----|:-----|
| The earthquake **had started** before people woke up. | 地震在人醒来之前就发生了 |
| The flood **had destroyed** the village by the time help arrived. | 救援到达时洪水已摧毁了村庄 |
| After the volcano **had erupted**, the sky turned dark. | 火山喷发后，天空变黑了 |
| They **had never experienced** a hurricane before. | 他们从未经历过飓风 |

### ⚠️ 注意

- Past Perfect 常和 **before / after / by the time / when / already / never** 一起用
- 如果时间顺序很清楚，也可以用 Simple Past
  - We **ate** dinner and then **watched** TV.（按时间顺序）

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| What can we learn from nature's power? | 我们能从自然力量中学到什么？ |
| The earthquake had started before we woke up. | 地震在我们醒来之前就开始了。 |
| By the time the firefighters arrived, the wildfire had spread. | 消防员到达时，野火已经蔓延了。 |
| After the volcano had erupted, the sky became dark. | 火山喷发后，天空变暗了。 |

| Hurricanes are one of the most powerful forces in nature. | 飓风是自然界最强大的力量之一。 |

## 🎧 Listening & Speaking 听说练习

- 听关于自然灾害的报道
- 口语练习：Describing what happened（描述发生了什么）
  - "What had happened before the rescue team came?"
  - "The flood had destroyed many houses."
  - "After the earthquake had stopped, people went outside."
- 用 Past Perfect 描述新闻事件

---

## ✍️ Writing 写作

- **Cause & Effect Paragraph**：写自然灾害的因果
  - 开头：A big hurricane hit the city last year.
  - 中间：Before it arrived, people had prepared. The wind had started blowing strongly…
  - 结尾：The hurricane had passed by morning. We learned that nature is very powerful.
- 用 4+ Past Perfect 句子
