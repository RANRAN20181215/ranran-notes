# 📘 Unit 13 What Is the Mass Media?

> Big Question 7: **What is the mass media?**
> 主题领域: Social Studies（社科/传媒）| 阅读策略: Fact vs Opinion（事实 vs 观点）
> 文体: **Nonfiction（信息文）**

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- **Mass media**（大众传媒）是传递信息给大量人的方式
- 最早的媒体是 **newspaper**（报纸）和 **magazine**（杂志）
- **Printing press**（印刷机）的发明让书本和报纸更便宜
- 后来有了 **radio**（收音机）和 **television**（电视）
- 现在最常用的是 **the Internet**（互联网）
- **Reporter**（记者）收集新闻，写 **article**（文章）
- **Interview**（采访）：记者问问题，得到信息
- 读文章时区分 Fact vs Opinion：
  - Fact：Newspapers are printed on paper.
  - Opinion：Newspapers are the best way to get news.

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Reported Speech (say & tell)

### 什么是 Reported Speech 间接引语？

| Direct Speech（直接引语） | Reported Speech（间接引语） |
|:-------------------------|:-------------------------|
| "I like this magazine," she said. | She **said (that)** she **liked** that magazine. |
| 原话：用引号 | 转述：不用引号，时态变化 |

### Say vs Tell

| 动词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|:----|:----|:-----|
| **say** | say + (that) + 句子 | He **said** (that) he was a reporter. |
| **tell** | tell + 人 + (that) + 句子 | He **told me** (that) he was a reporter. |

### 时态变化 Tense Changes

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|:--------------|:---------------|
| 一般现在时 | 一般过去时 |
| "I **work** for a newspaper." | He said he **worked** for a newspaper. |
| 现在进行时 | 过去进行时 |
| "I **am writing** an article." | She said she **was writing** an article. |
| 一般将来时 (will) | would |
| "I **will** interview the mayor." | He said he **would** interview the mayor. |
| can | could |
| "I **can** take photos." | She said she **could** take photos. |

### 其他变化

| Direct | Reported |
|:-------|:---------|
| this | that |
| these | those |
| here | there |
| now | then / at that time |
| today | that day |
| tomorrow | the next day |

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|

## 🎧 Listening & Speaking 听说练习

- 听记者采访的对话
- 口语练习：Reporting what someone said
  - "What did he say?"
  - "He said that he was a journalist."
  - "He told me that he worked for a magazine."
- 把直接引语转成间接引语

---

## ✍️ Writing 写作

- **News Report**：写一段新闻报道，然后转述
  - 第一部分（直接引语）：The mayor said, "Our city is growing."
  - 第二部分（Reported Speech）：The mayor said that their city was growing.
- 用 4+ Reported Speech 句子

## 🎮 Fun Activities 趣味活动

3. **📰 Make a Class Newspaper**：每人写一篇小新闻，用 Reported Speech
4. **🎭 Direct → Reported Match**：配对直接引语和间接引语卡片
