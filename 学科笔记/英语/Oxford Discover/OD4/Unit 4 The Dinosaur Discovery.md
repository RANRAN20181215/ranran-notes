# 📘 Unit 4 The Dinosaur Discovery

> Big Question 2: **How do we know what happened long ago?**
> 主题领域: Science（科学/古生物）| 阅读策略: Sequence（顺序）
> 文体: **Fiction（故事）**

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 故事讲的是孩子们发现恐龙化石的经历
- 他们在 **ravine**（峡谷）里发现了一个 **skull**（头骨）
- 一个 **paleontologist**（古生物学家）来 **examine**（检查）它
- 他们 **excavate**（挖掘）出更多骨头
- 化石藏在 **layers**（岩层）里，一层一层堆叠
- 有些化石在 **sedimentary rock**（沉积岩）里
- **Volcanic ash**（火山灰）帮助保存了化石
- 读故事时注意 Sequence：First → Next → Then → Finally

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Present Perfect (for Change Over Time)

### 过去发生，现在有影响

| 结构 | 例句 | 说明 |
|:----|:-----|:-----|
| **has / have** + v3 | The paleontologist **has found** a big bone. | 找到了（现在有了） |
| **has / have + never** | I **have never seen** a real dinosaur. | 从未做过 |
| **already** | They **have already excavated** the ravine. | 已经做了 |
| **just** | We **have just discovered** a new fossil! | 刚刚做 |

### 肯定句、否定句、疑问句

| 类型 | 例句 |
|:----|:-----|
| 肯定句 | The paleontologists **have found** a dinosaur skull. |
| 否定句 | They **haven't found** the whole skeleton yet. |
| 一般疑问句 | **Have** you **ever excavated** a fossil? |
| 肯定回答 | Yes, I **have**. |
| 否定回答 | No, I **haven't**. |
| 特殊疑问句 | What **have** they **discovered**? |

### Present Perfect vs Simple Past 对比

| 时态 | 用法 | 例句 |
|:----|:-----|:-----|
| **Present Perfect** | 强调经历，不强调时间 | We **have found** fossils here. |
| **Simple Past** | 强调具体时间 | We **found** this skull **yesterday**. |

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| The paleontologist has examined the dinosaur skull. | 古生物学家检查了恐龙头骨。 |
| Have you ever seen a dinosaur skeleton? | 你见过恐龙骨架吗？ |
| They haven't excavated the whole ravine yet. | 他们还没把整个峡谷挖完。 |
| The fossils have been buried for millions of years. | 这些化石被埋了数百万年。 |
| Dinosaurs have been extinct for a very long time. | 恐龙已经灭绝了很长时间。 |
| The skull was found in sedimentary rock. | 这个头骨是在沉积岩里发现的。 |

## 🎧 Listening & Speaking 听说练习

- 听关于恐龙化石发现的对话

  - "Have you ever been to a museum?"
  - "Have you ever seen a dinosaur fossil?"
  - "I have / haven't…"
- 用 **ever / never / already / yet** 谈论经历

---

## ✍️ Writing 写作

- **Paragraph about Discoveries**：用 Present Perfect 写一段发现经历
  - 开头：Last summer, I have visited a dinosaur museum.
  - 中间：I have seen a huge T-Rex skeleton. I have also touched a real fossil!
  - 结尾：I have learned a lot about dinosaurs.
- 用 **already / yet / never** 丰富句子
