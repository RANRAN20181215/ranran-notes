# 📘 Unit 16 Let's Make Music!

> Big Question 8: **How can we make music?**
> 阅读类型: 现实主义小说 | 阅读策略: Problems and Solutions

---

## 📖 Story 读一读

- 一群孩子想举办一场音乐会 (concert)
- 他们遇到了问题 (problem)：没有足够的乐器
- 一个孩子说："我有吉他"
- 另一个说："我会唱歌"
- 还有人说："我可以做沙锤"
- 他们分工合作：
  - 有人练习乐器 (practice an instrument)
  - 有人买票 (buy tickets)
  - 有人收钱 (give money)
  - 有人拍照 (take pictures)
- 最后音乐会非常成功，大家都鼓掌 (clap)
- 他们用创意解决了问题

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Present Continuous Questions

| 类型 | 问句 | 回答 |
|:----|:----|:----|
| 一般疑问句 | Is he playing the guitar? | Yes, he is. / No, he isn't. |
| 一般疑问句 | Are they singing? | Yes, they are. / No, they aren't. |
| 特殊疑问句 | What is she doing? | She is dancing. |
| 特殊疑问句 | What are you doing? | I am practicing. |

---

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |

## 🎧 Listening & Speaking 听说练习

- 听关于不同活动的观察描述
- 口语练习：Asking and Guessing（询问和猜测）
- 用 "What is he doing?" / "Is he playing the drum?" / "I think he is..."

---

## ✍️ Writing 写作

- 正确使用缩略形式 (contractions)
- I am → I'm / He is → He's / She is → She's / They are → They're
- 写正在进行的活动描述
- 示例：They're playing music. She's singing. He's dancing.

---

## 🎮 Fun Activities 趣味活动

1. **🎶 Music Charades**：做动作猜乐器或活动，用 "Is he playing the drum?"
2. **🎤 Concert Role-play**：模拟音乐会，一人介绍 "She is singing" / "They are playing"
3. **📸 Photo Description**：看音乐会的照片，用现在进行时描述
