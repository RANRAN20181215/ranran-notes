# 📘 Unit 15 Percussion Instruments

> Big Question 8: **How can we make music?**
> 阅读类型: 信息文（非虚构） | 阅读策略: Main Idea and Details

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 打击乐器 (percussion instruments) 是敲打发出声音的乐器
- **Drum**（鼓）：用手或鼓槌敲打鼓面
- **Tambourine**（铃鼓）：摇动或敲击，铃片会响
- **Xylophone**（木琴）：用槌敲击不同大小的木板，发出不同音高
- **Cymbals**（钹）：两片相撞发出响亮的声音
- **Triangle**（三角铁）：用金属棒敲击，发出清脆的声音
- 打击乐器让音乐有节奏 (rhythm)

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Present Continuous（现在进行时）

| 人称 | 肯定 | 否定 |
|:----|:----|:----|
| I am | I am playing the drum. | I am not playing the triangle. |
| He/She is | She is shaking the tambourine. | She isn't hitting the drum. |
| You/We/They are | They are playing the xylophone. | They aren't playing the cymbals. |

**现在分词 (-ing) 变化规则：**

| 规则 | 示例 |
|:----|:----|
| 一般 + ing | play → playing, hit → hitting |
| 以 e 结尾，去 e + ing | shake → shaking |
| 重读闭音节，双写 + ing | drum → drumming |

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| How can we make music? | 我们怎么创造音乐？ |
| I am playing the drum. | 我正在打鼓。 |
| She is shaking the tambourine. | 她正在摇铃鼓。 |
| He is striking the triangle. | 他正在敲三角铁。 |
| We are making music! | 我们在做音乐！ |
| The music is loud! | 音乐很大声！ |

## 🎧 Listening & Speaking 听说练习

- 听不同乐器的声音描述
- 口语练习：Asking for Help（请求帮助）
- 用 "Can you help me?" / "How do I play this?"

---

## ✍️ Writing 写作

- 用现在进行时描述正在发生的动作
- 练习：看一张演奏乐器的图，写 3 句描述
- 示例：The boy is playing the drum. The girl is shaking the tambourine.
