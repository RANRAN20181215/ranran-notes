# 📘 Unit 1 Families and Friends

> Big Question 1: **Who are your family and friends?**
> 阅读类型: 信息文（非虚构） | 阅读策略: Predicting from Pictures

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- A family is a group of people who love each other
- 家人住在一起，互相照顾
- 家庭成员包括：mother, father, brother, sister, grandmother, grandfather
- A friend is someone you like and enjoy being with
- 朋友是一起玩、一起分享的人
- 家人可以是朋友，朋友也可以像家人一样
- 每个人都有家人和朋友，他们让生活更美好

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Verb Be

| 人称 | 肯定 | 缩写 | 否定 |
|:----|:----|:----|:----|
| I | I am | I'm | I am not |
| You | You are | You're | You are not |
| He / She / It | He is | He's | He is not |
| We | We are | We're | We are not |
| They | They are | They're | They are not |

**例句:**

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| I am a student. | 我是一名学生。 |
| She is my mother. | 她是我的妈妈。 |
| They are my friends. | 他们是我的朋友。 |
| He is not my brother. | 他不是我的兄弟。 |

| He is my friend. | 他是我的朋友。 |
| We are friends. | 我们是朋友。 |
| I am seven years old. | 我七岁了。 |

---

## 🎧 Listening & Speaking 听说练习

- 听描述，指出对应的人物关系
- 口语练习：Introducing People（介绍他人）
- 用 "This is..." / "He is..." / "She is..." 介绍家人和朋友

---

## ✍️ Writing 写作

- 书写家人和朋友的名字
- 用 "I am..." / "You are..." / "She is..." 写简单句子
- 注意：人名首字母大写

---

## 🎮 Fun Activities 趣味活动

1. **👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Tree**：画家谱树，用 "He is my father / She is my sister" 介绍
2. **🤝 Friend Collage**：做朋友拼贴画，用 "We are friends" / "She is my friend" 写标签
3. **🎭 Role-play**：用 "This is..." 互相介绍家人照片
