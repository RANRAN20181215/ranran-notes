# 📘 Unit 7 The Four Seasons

> Big Question 4: **How are seasons different?**
> 阅读类型: 信息文（非虚构） | 阅读策略: Captions

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 一年有四个季节：spring, summer, autumn (fall), winter
- 每个季节有不同的天气和感受
- **Spring**：温暖 (warm)，花开了，白天变长
- **Summer**：炎热 (hot)，白天最长，可以游泳
- **Autumn (Fall)**：凉爽 (cool)，树叶变黄落下
- **Winter**：寒冷 (cold)，白天最短，有时下雪
- 每个季节都有自己的特点和美丽之处

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Simple Present with It

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|:----|
| It + is + 形容词 | It is warm in spring. | 春天是温暖的。 |
| It + rains/snows | It rains in spring. | 春天会下雨。 |
| 否定 | It isn't cold in summer. | 夏天不冷。 |
| 疑问 | Is it hot in summer? | 夏天热吗？ |

---

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| How are seasons different? | 季节有什么不同？ |
| It is warm in spring. | 春天天气温暖。 |
| It is hot and sunny in summer. | 夏天炎热晴朗。 |
| It is cold and snowy in winter. | 冬天寒冷下雪。 |
| In autumn, leaves fall from the trees. | 秋天，树叶从树上落下。 |
| Days are long in summer. | 夏天白天长。 |
| Days are short in winter. | 冬天白天短。 |

## 🎧 Listening & Speaking 听说练习

- 听关于不同季节天气的对话
- 口语练习：Making Invitations（发出邀请）
- 用 "Let's go to the beach in summer!" / "Do you want to make a snowman?"

---

## ✍️ Writing 写作

- 用 It is + 形容词 / It rains/snows 写季节描述
- 写一段最喜欢的季节，给出理由
- 示例：My favorite season is spring. It is warm. Flowers grow.

---

## 🎮 Fun Activities 趣味活动

1. **📅 Season Wheel**：做四季转盘，用 "In spring, it is..." / "In winter, it is..."
2. **🌡️ Weather Chart**：记录一周天气，用 "It is sunny / cloudy / rainy"
3. **🎨 Season Drawing**：画最喜欢的季节并写 3 句描述
