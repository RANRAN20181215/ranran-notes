# 📘 Unit 12 Wants and Needs

> Big Question 6: **What do we need?**
> 阅读类型: 信息文（非虚构） | 阅读策略: Predicting from Titles, Headings, and Pictures

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- Wants 是你想要但非必需的东西
- Needs 是你必须有的东西才能生存
- **Needs**：water（水），food（食物），clothes（衣服），a home（家）
- **Wants**：toys, games, sweets, new shoes
- 不同的人有不同的职业来满足 needs
- 医生 (doctor)、警察 (police officer)、老师 (teacher) 都在帮助别人
- 我们需要学会区分 wants 和 needs
- 这帮助我们把钱花在真正重要的东西上

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Simple Present Questions with Regular Verbs and Have (He, She)

| 类型 | 问句 | 回答 |
|:----|:----|:----|
| Does + he/she + 动词? | Does he need water? | Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't. |
| Does + he/she + have? | Does she have a job? | Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't. |
| What + does + he/she + need? | What does she need? | She needs food and water. |

**对比：**

| 主语 | 陈述句 | 疑问句 |
|:----|:-------|:-------|
| He | He needs water. | Does he need water? |
| She | She wants a new dress. | Does she want a new dress? |
| They | They need a home. | Do they need a home? |

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| What do we need? | 我们需要什么？ |
| We need water and food. | 我们需要水和食物。 |
| She wants a new doll. | 她想要一个新娃娃。 |
| Does he need a doctor? | 他需要看医生吗？ |
| A doctor helps people. | 医生帮助人们。 |
| Wants are not the same as needs. | 想要和需要不一样。 |

## 🎧 Listening & Speaking 听说练习

- 听关于野餐的对话
- 口语练习：Expressing Wants and Needs（表达想要和需要）
- 用 "I need..." / "I want..." / "Do you need...?"

---

## ✍️ Writing 写作

- 区分名词 (noun) 和动词 (verb)
- 写 3 个关于 needs 的句子和 3 个关于 wants 的句子
- 用 "I need water. I want a toy."

---

## 🎮 Fun Activities 趣味活动

1. **🛒 Needs vs Wants Sort**：把卡片分类到 Needs 和 Wants 两栏
2. **🧑‍⚕️ Job Role-play**：扮演不同职业，用 "I help people. I am a doctor."
3. **📋 Survey**：调查同学 "Do you need...? / Do you want...?" 并记录结果
