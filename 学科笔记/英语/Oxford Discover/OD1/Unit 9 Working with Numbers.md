# 📘 Unit 9 Working with Numbers

> Big Question 5: **How do numbers help us?**
> 阅读类型: 信息文（非虚构） | 阅读策略: Predicting from Headings

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 数字每天都在帮助我们
- 我们数东西、做加法、看时间、用钱
- 奇数和偶数 (odd and even numbers) 有不同的规律
- 加法：用 plus sign (+)，等于用 equals sign (=)
- 示例：3 + 2 = 5，8 + 1 = 9
- 数字让生活有秩序，帮助我们理解世界

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Simple Present with Have (I, You, We, They)

| 人称 | 肯定 | 否定 |
|:----|:----|:----|
| I | I have a pen. | I don't have a ruler. |
| You | You have a notebook. | You don't have an eraser. |
| We | We have five pencils. | We don't have a backpack. |
| They | They have ten erasers. | They don't have a pen. |

---

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| How do numbers help us? | 数字怎么帮助我们？ |
| I have three pencils. | 我有三支铅笔。 |
| We have two notebooks. | 我们有两个笔记本。 |
| 3 + 2 = 5 | Three plus two equals five. |
| 2 is an even number. | 2 是偶数。 |
| 3 is an odd number. | 3 是奇数。 |
| I don't have a ruler. | 我没有尺子。 |

## 🎧 Listening & Speaking 听说练习

- 听关于加法和数字的讨论
- 口语练习：Asking and Telling Age / Talking About Addition
- 用 "How old are you?" / "I am seven." / "What is 3 + 2?"

---

## ✍️ Writing 写作

- 写句子描述学习用品和数量
- 用 "I have..." / "I don't have..."
- 示例：I have three pencils and two notebooks. I don't have a ruler.

---

## 🎮 Fun Activities 趣味活动

1. **🔢 Number Hunt**：在教室里找到数字并用 "I have..." 说出来
2. **🧮 Math Race**：两人比赛做简单加法，用英语说出算式
3. **🎒 School Bag Count**：互相检查书包里的文具，用 "I have... / I don't have..."
