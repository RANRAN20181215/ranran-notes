# 📘 Unit 8 Doctor Molly

> Big Question 4: **How do we measure time?**
> 主题领域: Science / Math（科学/数学）| 阅读策略: Predict

---

## 📖 Story 读一读

- Molly 想当医生 (doctor)
- 为了成为医生，她有很多事情要做：
  - She **is going to** study hard
  - She **is going to** go to medical school
  - She **is going to** help sick people
- 但是她现在还有时间！
  - 今天下午她 **is going to** 做作业
  - 明天她 **is going to** 去图书馆看书
  - 下周她 **is going to** 参观医院
- **Predicting**（预测）：
  - 边读边猜接下来会发生什么
  - 用图片和标题来帮助预测

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Future Plans with Going to

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|:----|
| **am/is/are going to** + 动词 | She **is going to** be a doctor. | 她打算成为一名医生。 |
| **am/is/are going to** + 动词 | I **am going to** study science. | 我打算学科学。 |
| **am not / isn't / aren't going to** | He **isn't going to** give up. | 他不打算放弃。 |
| **Am/Is/Are** + 主语 + **going to**? | **Are** you **going to** help me? | 你打算帮我吗？ |

**Will vs Going to：**

| Will（事实/预测） | Going to（计划/意图） |
|:-----------------|:---------------------|
| The sun **will rise**. | I **am going to** study. |
| It **will** be dark. | She **is going to** be a doctor. |
| 客观会发生的事 | 主观打算做的事 |
