# 📘 Unit 10 Hector, the Energy Saver!

> Big Question 5: **Where does energy come from?**
> 主题领域: Science（科学）| 阅读策略: Draw Conclusions

---

## 📖 Story 读一读

- Hector 是个普通的男孩
- 他发现家里浪费了很多能源：
  - Lights were on in empty rooms
  - TV was on but no one was watching
  - Water was running for too long
- Hector 开始做 **energy saver**（节能卫士）：
  - 他关掉不用的灯
  - 他关掉不看的电视
  - 他缩短洗澡时间
- 他的家人也加入了他的行动
- **Draw Conclusions**（得出结论）：
  - Fact：Hector 关掉了不用的灯
  - Fact：电费变少了
  - Conclusion：节约能源可以省钱

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Quantifiers 量词

| 量词 | 可数名词 | 不可数名词 |
|:----|:--------|:----------|
| **a lot of** | a lot of lights | a lot of energy |
| **some** | some bulbs | some water |
| **a few** | a few lights | — |
| **a little** | — | a little energy |
| **not many** | not many lights | — |
| **not much** | — | not much energy |
| **any** (否定/疑问) | any lights? | any energy? |

**例句：**

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| There are **a lot of** lights in the house. | 房子里有很多灯。 |
| There is **some** water running. | 有一些水在流。 |
| He uses **a little** energy for the TV. | 他看电视用一点电。 |
| There aren't **many** lights on. | 没有很多灯开着。 |
| There isn't **much** time. | 没有多少时间了。 |
