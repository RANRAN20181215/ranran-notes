# 📘 Unit 3 Letters to a Friend

> Big Question 2: **Why do people move to new places?**
> 主题领域: Social Studies, Geography（社科·地理）| 阅读策略: Compare and Contrast

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 故事通过两封 **letter**（信）讲述了两个孩子的搬家经历
- **Ana** 从城市搬到了郊区 (suburb)：
  - Before：住城里，可以去 shopping mall
  - After：搬到郊区，有更大院子，可以骑自行车
  - She **could** go to the mall before
  - Now she **can** ride her bike to school
- **Ben** 搬到了新的国家：
  - 新国家的天气不同 (monsoon 雨季)
  - 吃不同的食物 (curry)
  - 认识了新的 classmate
- **Compare and Contrast**（比较和对比）：
  - Same：都是搬家，都认识了新朋友
  - Different：Ana 搬到郊区，Ben 搬到新国家

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Can and Could

| 时态 | 用法 | 例句 |
|:----|:----|:----|
| **Can** | 现在的能力 | I **can** ride a bike. |
| **Can't** | 现在不能 | He **can't** swim. |
| **Could** | 过去的能力 | I **could** read when I was five. |
| **Couldn't** | 过去不能 | She **couldn't** speak English before. |

**对比：**

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| Now, I **can** ride my bike to school. | 现在，我可以骑自行车去上学。 |
| Before, I **could** go to the mall. | 以前，我可以去商场。 |
| I **couldn't** swim when I was little. | 我小时候不会游泳。 |
| In my new country, I **can** eat curry. | 在新国家，我可以吃咖喱。 |

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| Why do people move to new places? | 人们为什么搬到新地方？ |
| People move for many reasons. | 人们搬家有很多原因。 |
| I can ride a bike in my new town. | 我在新城镇可以骑自行车。 |
| Before, I could go to the mall. | 以前，我可以去商场。 |
| Ana moved to the suburb. | Ana 搬到了郊区。 |
| Ben moved to a different country. | Ben 搬到了不同的国家。 |
| My new classmate is from Mexico. | 我的新同学来自墨西哥。 |
