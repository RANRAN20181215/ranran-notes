# BQ1: Why do we protect animals? — Unit 2 F

## Big Question
Why do we protect animals?

## Reading Genre
Fiction — a story about a person connecting with animals

## Vocabulary Review
Apply Unit 1 vocab in a narrative context: habitat, sanctuary, tracker, inspire, survive, preserve

## Grammar in Context: Present Perfect Continuous

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Recent action with evidence | *Her boots are muddy. She has been walking through the forest.* |
| Action that started and continues | *They have been living at the sanctuary since last year.* |
| Repeated action over time | *The naturalist has been visiting the reserve every month.* |

## Word Study: Compound Nouns
- wildlife, rainforest, ecosystem, fieldwork, greenhouse
- *wild + life = wildlife*
- *rain + forest = rainforest*

## Writing: Persuasive Essay
Structure:
1. **Introduction** — state your opinion (I believe we should protect …)
2. **Body** — 2–3 reasons with evidence
3. **Conclusion** — restate opinion and call to action

### Persuasive language
- *We must … because …*
- *If we don't …, then …*
- *It is important to …*

## Example Sentences
> She has been working as a tracker for five years.
> They have already saved three endangered species.
> The reserve has been protecting wildlife since 2005.
