# BQ2: What are teeth for? — Unit 4 F

## Big Question
What are teeth for?

## Reading Genre
Fiction — a story about visiting the dentist or losing a tooth

## Vocabulary Review
Apply Unit 3 vocab in narrative: cavity, enamel, gums, jaw, dental, incisor, molar

## Grammar in Context: Modals of Ability

| Modal | Past | Present | Future |
|---|---|---|---|
| can/could | *could* | *can* | — |
| be able to | *was/were able to* | *am/is/are able to* | *will be able to* |

### Ability + condition
- *If you brush your teeth, you will be able to prevent cavities.*
- *She could eat apples again after her filling.*

## Word Study: Prefix un-
- **un** + healthy → unhealthy
- **un** + able → unable
- **un** + comfortable → uncomfortable
- **un** + necessary → unnecessary

## Writing: A Personal Narrative
1. **Beginning** — set the scene (*Last Tuesday, I went to the dentist …*)
2. **Middle** — describe what happened (using past tenses)
3. **End** — how you felt and what you learned

### Time words
*first, then, after that, finally, later, the next day*

## Example Sentences
> I was able to stay calm during the dental check-up.
> My little sister can already brush her teeth by herself.
> When I was younger, I could not eat crunchy food with my loose tooth.
