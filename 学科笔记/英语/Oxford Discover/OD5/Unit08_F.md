# BQ4: How do animals communicate? — Unit 8 F

## Big Question
How do animals communicate?

## Reading Genre
Fiction — a story about a person learning to understand animal communication

## Vocabulary Review
Apply Unit 7 vocab in narrative: communicate, signal, gesture, warning, territory, interpret, display

## Grammar in Context: Passive Voice

| Tense | Example |
|---|---|
| Present Simple | *The dog is walked every morning.* (unimportant who) |
| Past Simple | *The message was sent through vibrations.* |
| Present Perfect | *The dolphins have been trained to respond to signals.* |
| Future | *A new study will be published about their communication.* |

### Active → Passive transformation
- Active: *Bees perform a waggle dance.*
- Passive: *A waggle dance is performed by bees.*

### Avoiding unnecessary passive
Use active when the doer is important (*Dolphins use clicks.* Not: *Clicks are used by dolphins.*)

## Word Study: Suffix -tion / -sion
- communicate → communication
- vibrate → vibration
- interpret → interpretation
- confuse → confusion
- decide → decision

## Writing: A Story from an Animal's Perspective
Write a short story narrated by an animal, showing how it communicates

### Sensory language
*sound, movement, light, smell, touch, vibration*

## Example Sentences
> The colony was warned about danger by a special dance.
> The meaning of the song had been interpreted incorrectly.
> Different gestures are used by chimpanzees to greet each other.
