# BQ7: Why do we use money? — Unit 14 F

## Big Question
Why do we use money?

## Reading Genre
Fiction — a story about earning, saving, or spending money

## Vocabulary Review
Apply Unit 13 vocab in narrative: save, budget, purchase, value, income, expense, invest, interest

## Grammar in Context: Reported Speech

| Direct (story dialogue) | Reported |
|---|---|
| "I need more money." | *He said he needed more money.* |
| "Are you saving?" | *She asked if I was saving.* |
| "Don't waste it!" | *He told me not to waste it.* |
| "Where did you buy it?" | *She asked where I had bought it.* |

### Questions in reported speech
- Yes/No: *"Is it expensive?" → She asked if it was expensive.*
- WH: *"How much did it cost?" → He asked how much it had cost.*
- No question mark, no auxiliary inversion

### Commands/Requests
- *"Save your money" → He told me to save my money.*
- *"Don't spend it all" → She told me not to spend it all.*

## Word Study: Homophones (words that sound the same)
- **sale** / **sail**
- **buy** / **by** / **bye**
- **Sell** / **cell**
- **cent** / **sent** / **scent**
- **waste** / **waist**

## Writing: A Dialogue-Based Story
1. Write a conversation about money
2. Rewrite part of it in reported speech
3. Use a mix of said, told, asked, replied, explained

### Reporting verbs
*explained, admitted, complained, suggested, reminded*

## Example Sentences
> She said that she had spent all her allowance.
> He asked me if I had saved enough for the purchase.
> Mom reminded me not to buy things I did not need.
