# BQ8: How do we express ourselves? — Unit 15 NF

## Big Question
How do we express ourselves?

## Vocabulary
- **express** — show thoughts or feelings
- **art** — the use of creativity to express ideas
- **painting** — a picture made with paint
- **sculpture** — a 3D work of art
- **music** — sounds arranged in patterns
- **poetry** — writing that expresses feelings in a special style
- **acting** — performing a role in a play or film
- **dance** — moving the body in a rhythmic way
- **theater** — a building where plays are performed
- **performance** — a show for an audience
- **interpretation** — a personal understanding or version
- **emotion** — a strong feeling
- **audience** — the people watching a performance

## Reading Strategy
Taking notes — capturing key points from informational text

## Grammar: Question Tags

| Statement | Tag | Example |
|---|---|---|
| + verb, − tag | − | *You like art, **don't you**?* |
| − verb, + tag | + | *You don't paint, **do you**?* |
| is/isn't | isn't/is | *It's beautiful, **isn't it**?* |
| can/can't | can't/can | *She can act, **can't she**?* |
| will/won't | won't/will | *They'll perform, **won't they**?* |

### Pronunciation
- Rising intonation = real question (*You like jazz, ↑ don't you?*)
- Falling intonation = expecting agreement (*It's amazing, ↓ isn't it?*)

## Grammar: Quantifiers
- **countable**: (not) enough actors, too many scenes, a few paintings
- **uncountable**: (not) enough music, too much noise, a little talent
- *There is too much dialogue in this play.*
- *There aren't enough dancers for the performance.*

## Writing Focus
A descriptive review of a performance or artwork

## Example Sentences
> That painting is stunning, isn't it?
> You enjoy poetry, don't you?
> There isn't enough emotion in this performance, is there?
