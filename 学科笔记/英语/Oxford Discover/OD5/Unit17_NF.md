# BQ9: Why do we make buildings? — Unit 17 NF

## Big Question
Why do we make buildings?

## Vocabulary
- **architecture** — the art of designing buildings
- **structure** — something built; a framework
- **foundation** — the base that supports a building
- **material** — what something is made from
- **concrete** — a hard building material
- **steel** — a strong metal used in buildings
- **beam** — a long, strong piece supporting weight
- **column** — a vertical support pillar
- **dome** — a round roof shaped like a bowl
- **arch** — a curved structure over an opening
- **engineer** — a person who designs structures
- **design** — a plan or drawing of a building
- **construct** — build something large
- **shelter** — a place that protects you

## Reading Strategy
Summarizing — identifying the main idea and key details

## Grammar: Purpose Clauses

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| to + infinitive | *We build houses **to** protect ourselves.* |
| in order to + infinitive | *Roman engineers used arches **in order to** support heavy walls.* |
| so that + clause | *They designed wide columns **so that** the building would stand for centuries.* |
| for + noun | *Concrete is used **for** strength.* |

### so that — different subject possible
- *I built a shelter **so that** my family **could** stay safe.* (I → my family)

### Negative purpose
- *to avoid + noun / so that + not*
- *They reinforced the walls **to avoid** collapse.*
- *They added beams **so that** the roof **would not** fall.*

## Writing Focus
A descriptive essay about a famous building or structure

## Example Sentences
> Skyscrapers need steel frames to support their weight.
> The architect used glass so that more light could enter the building.
> Engineers designed the foundation to prevent sinking.
