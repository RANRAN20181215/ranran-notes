# BQ7: Why do we use money? — Unit 13 NF

## Big Question
Why do we use money?

## Vocabulary
- **currency** — the money used in a country
- **trade** — exchange goods or services
- **barter** — exchange without money
- **coin** — a flat piece of metal used as money
- **banknote** — paper money
- **value** — how much something is worth
- **economy** — the system of money and trade
- **invest** — put money in something to earn more
- **save** — keep money for later
- **budget** — a plan for spending money
- **income** — money received (salary, wages)
- **expense** — money spent
- **interest** — money paid for borrowing or saving
- **purchase** — buy something

## Reading Strategy
Distinguishing fact from opinion in economic texts

## Grammar: Reported Speech

| Direct speech | Reported speech |
|---|---|
| "I save money." | *She said (that) she saved money.* |
| "I am saving." | *He said (that) he was saving.* |
| "I have saved." | *They said (that) they had saved.* |
| "I will save." | *She said (that) she would save.* |
| "I can save." | *He said (that) he could save.* |
| "I must save." | *They said (that) they had to save.* |

### Tense shift (backshift)
- present → past
- past → past perfect
- will → would
- can → could
- must → had to

### Say vs Tell
- say + (that) + clause (*He said that money was important.*)
- tell + person + (that) + clause (*She told me that the economy was growing.*)

## Writing Focus
An opinion essay about the importance of saving money

## Example Sentences
> The economist said that barter was the oldest form of trade.
> He told us that coins had been used for over 2,000 years.
> She said she would invest her savings in the future.
