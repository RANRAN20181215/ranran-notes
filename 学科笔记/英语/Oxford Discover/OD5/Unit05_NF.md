# BQ3: Why are wheels important? — Unit 5 NF

## Big Question
Why are wheels important?

## Vocabulary
- **axle** — a rod that passes through a wheel's center
- **rotate** — turn around a central point
- **spokes** — bars connecting wheel center to outer edge
- **transportation** — moving people or goods
- **goods** — items that are bought or sold
- **vehicle** — a machine that carries people or things
- **mill** — a machine that grinds grain
- **grind** — crush into small pieces
- **friction** — resistance when two surfaces rub
- **bearing** — a part that reduces friction in a wheel
- **pulley** — a wheel used to lift heavy objects
- **gear** — a toothed wheel that transfers motion
- **cart** — a vehicle pulled by animals

## Reading Strategy
Sequencing events — how wheel technology developed over time

## Grammar: Past Perfect

| Form | Example |
|---|---|
| had + past participle | *People had used logs as rollers before they invented the wheel.* |

### Past Perfect vs Simple Past
- Simple Past: one past event *(They invented the wheel.)*
- Past Perfect: the earlier of two past events *(They had used sleds before they invented the wheel.)*

### Signal words
- by the time, before, after, already, just, never, when

### Timeline
```
[had used logs] -------- [invented wheel] -------- [NOW]
   Past Perfect             Simple Past
```

## Writing Focus
An explanatory paragraph about how wheels changed transportation

## Example Sentences
> By 3500 BCE, people had already built wheeled carts in Mesopotamia.
> Before the wheel was invented, people had carried goods on their backs.
> The Egyptians had used rollers for years before they created the spoked wheel.
