# BQ3: Why are wheels important? — Unit 6 F

## Big Question
Why are wheels important?

## Reading Genre
Fiction — a story about a journey involving a vehicle or wheeled transport

## Vocabulary Review
Apply Unit 5 vocab in narrative: axle, rotate, broke down, transportation, cart, goods, vehicle

## Grammar in Context: Past Perfect

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Past before another past | *The cart broke down because a spoke had snapped.* |
| Past perfect with before/after | *After they had fixed the axle, they continued the journey.* |
| Past perfect with by the time | *By the time they arrived, the market had already closed.* |

### Contrast
- *The wagon broke down.* (just stating what happened)
- *The wagon broke down because the axle had cracked.* (explaining cause)

## Word Study: Prefix re-
- **re** + build → rebuild
- **re** + invent → reinvent
- **re** + place → replace
- **re** + pair → repair *(already has re-)*

## Writing: A Story with Flashback
1. Start in the present of the story
2. Use Past Perfect to show earlier events
3. Return to the main timeline with Simple Past

### Transition words for sequence
*Before that, Earlier, Previously, When they finally …*

## Example Sentences
> The spokes had bent, so the wheel was wobbly.
> She realized she had forgotten the spare parts.
> By the time help arrived, they had fixed the wheel themselves.
