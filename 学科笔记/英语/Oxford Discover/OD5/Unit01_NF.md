# BQ1: Why do we protect animals? — Unit 1 NF

## Big Question
Why do we protect animals?

## Vocabulary
- **extinct** — no longer existing
- **endangered** — at risk of becoming extinct
- **habitat** — natural home of an animal or plant
- **species** — a group of living things that are similar
- **diverse** — showing a great deal of variety
- **survive** — continue to live
- **naturalist** — a person who studies nature
- **tracker** — a person who follows animal signs
- **poaching** — illegal hunting
- **reserve** — protected area for wildlife
- **sanctuary** — a safe place for animals
- **population** — number of living things in an area
- **preserve** — protect from harm
- **inspire** — fill with the urge to do something

## Reading Strategy
Taking notes — main ideas and supporting details

## Grammar: Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous

| Present Perfect | Present Perfect Continuous |
|---|---|
| Action completed (result) | Action in progress (duration) |
| *Scientists have discovered a new species.* | *They have been studying the habitat for years.* |
| Focus: **result / how many** | Focus: **duration / how long** |

### Signal words
- **PP**: already, yet, just, ever, never, recently, so far
- **PPC**: for, since, all day/week, how long, lately

### Form
- PP: have/has + past participle
- PPC: have/has + been + verb-ing

### Key distinction
- *I have read the report.* (= finished)
- *I have been reading the report.* (= still reading)

## Writing Focus
A persuasive paragraph about protecting an endangered animal

## Example Sentences
> Many species have become extinct because of habitat loss.
> Conservationists have been protecting the reserve for decades.
> How long have you been tracking this population?
