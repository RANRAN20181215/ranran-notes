# BQ6: Why are mountains important? — Unit 12 F

## Big Question
Why are mountains important?

## Reading Genre
Fiction — a story set in the mountains (adventure / survival)

## Vocabulary Review
Apply Unit 11 vocab in narrative: peak, summit, glacier, altitude, ecosystem, climate, range

## Grammar in Context: Conditionals

| Type | Story Context |
|---|---|
| 1 | *If we reach the summit by sunset, we will set up camp.* |
| 2 | *If I had better equipment, I would attempt the climb.* |
| 3 | *If the storm had not come, they would have reached the peak.* |

### Wishes with if only / I wish
- *I wish I was at the base camp right now.* (Type 2)
- *If only we had brought more oxygen.* (Type 3)

## Word Study: Suffix -ous
- mountain → mountainous
- danger → dangerous
- fame → famous
- courage → courageous
- nerve → nervous

## Writing: A Survival Story
Write a story about a challenging mountain experience

### Story arc
1. **Setting** — describe the mountain (altitude, weather, terrain)
2. **Problem** — a difficulty (bad weather, injury, getting lost)
3. **Climax** — the most tense moment
4. **Resolution** — how the problem was solved

### Suspense techniques
*Suddenly, Without warning, At that moment, If only …*

## Example Sentences
> If the glacier had not cracked, they would have crossed safely.
> I wish I had brought warmer clothes — the altitude is freezing.
> If we see a bear, we will stay very still.
