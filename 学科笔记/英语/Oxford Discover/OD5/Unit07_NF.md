# BQ4: How do animals communicate? — Unit 7 NF

## Big Question
How do animals communicate?

## Vocabulary
- **communicate** — share information
- **signal** — a sign that sends a message
- **gesture** — a movement that communicates
- **vibration** — rapid back-and-forth movement
- **pheromone** — a chemical signal animals release
- **echolocation** — using sound waves to locate objects
- **bioluminescence** — producing light (e.g. fireflies)
- **display** — a show to attract or warn
- **courtship** — behavior to attract a mate
- **territory** — an area an animal defends
- **warning** — a signal of danger
- **colony** — a group of the same species living together
- **interpret** — explain the meaning of

## Reading Strategy
Comparing and contrasting — how different animals communicate

## Grammar: Passive Voice

| Tense | Active | Passive |
|---|---|---|
| Present Simple | *Bees dance to share information.* | *Information is shared by bees.* |
| Past Simple | *The bee performed a dance.* | *A dance was performed.* |
| Present Perfect | *Scientists have discovered a new signal.* | *A new signal has been discovered.* |
| Future | *Researchers will study the behavior.* | *The behavior will be studied.* |

### Form
- be (conjugated) + past participle
- by + agent (optional)

### When to use passive
- The doer is unknown (*The message was sent.*)
- The action is more important (*Echolocation is used by dolphins.*)
- The doer is obvious (*Honey is made by bees.*)

## Writing Focus
A compare and contrast essay about animal communication methods

## Example Sentences
> Pheromones are released by ants to mark trails.
> Echolocation is used by bats to find food in the dark.
> Many warning signals have been studied by biologists.
