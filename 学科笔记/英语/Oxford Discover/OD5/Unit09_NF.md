# BQ5: What do different cultures give to the world? — Unit 9 NF

## Big Question
What do different cultures give to the world?

## Vocabulary
- **culture** — the customs and beliefs of a group
- **tradition** — a long-established custom
- **contribution** — something given or offered
- **civilization** — an advanced stage of human society
- **ancient** — very old; from long ago
- **custom** — a traditional practice
- **cuisine** — a style of cooking
- **philosophy** — the study of knowledge and existence
- **astronomy** — the study of stars and planets
- **medicine** — the science of treating illness
- **calendars** — systems for measuring days and years
- **invention** — something created for the first time
- **architecture** — the art of designing buildings

## Reading Strategy
Making connections — text to self, text to world, text to text

## Grammar: Relative Clauses

| Pronoun | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **who** | people | *The scholar **who** invented the calendar was from Egypt.* |
| **which** | things/animals | *The cuisine **which** spread worldwide came from China.* |
| **that** | people or things | *The invention **that** changed the world was paper.* |
| **where** | places | *This is the city **where** philosophy was born.* |
| **whose** | possession | *The culture **whose** alphabet we use is Greek.* |

### Defining (no commas) vs Non-defining (with commas)
- Defining: *The invention **that** changed everything was the wheel.* (essential info)
- Non-defining: *Paper, **which** was invented in China, spread along the Silk Road.* (extra info, needs commas)

## Writing Focus
A research report about one culture's contribution to the world

## Example Sentences
> The ancient Greeks, who developed democracy, influenced many countries.
> Paper, which was invented in China, changed how we share knowledge.
> This is the library where ancient manuscripts were kept.
