# BQ8: How do we express ourselves? — Unit 16 F

## Big Question
How do we express ourselves?

## Reading Genre
Fiction — a story about a young artist, musician, or performer

## Vocabulary Review
Apply Unit 15 vocab in narrative: express, art, performance, theater, emotion, audience, interpretation, dance, acting

## Grammar in Context: Question Tags

| Statement | Tag |
|---|---|
| *She performed beautifully, **didn't she**?* | expecting agreement |
| *You haven't seen this play, **have you**?* | genuine question |
| *Let's watch a ballet, **shall we**?* | suggestion |
| *Don't be nervous, **will you**?* | polite command |

### Common mistakes
- ❌ *He is an actor, isn't he?* → ✅ *He is an actor, isn't he?* ✓
- ❌ *You come to theater, don't you?* → ✅ *You come to theater, don't you?* ✓

### Intonation practice
- *Rising* (↑) = asking, uncertain
- *Falling* (↓) = confirming, certain

## Word Study: Verb + Preposition
- believe **in** (your talent)
- dream **of** (becoming an actor)
- work **on** (a performance)
- prepare **for** (an audition)
- succeed **in** (a career)
- depend **on** (your team)

## Writing: A Personal Narrative about Performance
Tell a story about a time you performed or expressed yourself

### Structure
1. Before — preparation, nervousness
2. During — what happened on stage
3. After — how you felt, what you learned

### Sensory details
*the bright lights, the silent audience, the pounding heart, the final applause*

## Example Sentences
> The audience loved the performance, didn't they?
> You have been practicing for weeks, haven't you?
> She believed in her talent, so she succeeded in the audition.
