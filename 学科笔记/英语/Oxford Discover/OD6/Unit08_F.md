### Word Study
Suffix **-ment** (noun-forming from verbs):
- *arrange → arrangement, achieve → achievement, develop → development*

### Grammar: Wishes and Regrets

**Form**:

| Structure | Use | Example |
|-----------|-----|---------|
| wish + past simple | want something to be different now | *I wish every snowflake lasted longer.* |
| wish + past perfect | regret about the past | *She wished she had studied the pattern more carefully.* |
| if only + past / past perfect | stronger wish / regret | *If only we could capture the beauty of snowflakes!* |

**Key difference**: **wish** ≠ **hope** — *wish* is for unreal/imaginary situations; *hope* is for possible ones
