## Unit 13 (F) — The Poetry of Birds

### BQ7: What makes birds special?

**Genre**: Poetry / Fiction
**Reading Strategy**: Visualize

### Vocabulary

| Word | POS | Definition |
|------|-----|------------|
| argue | v | to quarrel with words |
| amuse | v | to cause to smile or laugh |
| lazily | adv | in a way that avoids effort |
| awkward | adj | lacking physical skill or grace |
| pitifully | adv | in a way that makes people feel sympathy |
| clumsy | adj | lacking grace in movement |
| limp | v | to walk with difficulty |
| soar | v | to fly high in the air |
| glide | v | to move smoothly and continuously |

**Words in Context**: feather, nest, wing, beak, song, flight

### Word Study
Suffix **-ly** (adverb-forming):
- *lazy → lazily, clumsy → clumsily, pitiful → pitifully*
- *quick → quickly, careful → carefully*

### Grammar: Gerunds and Infinitives

**Gerund** (verb + -ing used as a noun):
- *Birds enjoy **soaring** through the sky.*
- **Swimming** is a skill some birds have.

**Infinitive** (to + base verb):
- *Birds need **to migrate** when winter comes.*
- *It is important **to protect** bird habitats.*

**Verbs that take gerund**: enjoy, avoid, suggest, finish, imagine, keep
**Verbs that take infinitive**: want, need, hope, decide, promise, learn
**Verbs that take both** (with meaning change):
- *remember doing* / *remember to do*
- *stop doing* / *stop to do*
