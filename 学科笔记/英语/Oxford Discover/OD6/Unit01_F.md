### Grammar: Future Continuous

**Form**: will + be + verb-ing
- Affirmative: *By winter, the villagers will be finishing the tunnel.*
- Negative: *They won't be working during the storm.*
- Questions: *Will you be crossing the bridge tomorrow?*

**Use**: actions in progress at a specific future time; planned future events
