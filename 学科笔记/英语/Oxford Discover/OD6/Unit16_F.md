## Unit 16 (F) — Gripped by Fear

### BQ8: What are we afraid of?

**Genre**: Fiction (Adventure / Suspense)
**Reading Strategy**: Predict

### Vocabulary

| Word | POS | Definition |
|------|-----|------------|
| grip | v | to hold tightly |
| tremble | v | to shake involuntarily |
| panic | n | sudden uncontrollable fear |
| frozen | adj | unable to move due to fear |
| dread | n | great fear or apprehension |
| confront | v | to face up to and deal with |
| courage | n | the ability to do something frightening |
| suspense | n | a state of excited uncertainty |
| relief | n | a feeling of reassurance after anxiety |
| bravery | n | courageous behavior |
| shudder | v | to tremble with fear or cold |
| overcome | v | to prevail over something |

**Words in Context**: darkness, footsteps, heartbeat, whisper, shadow, escape

### Word Study
Prefix **un-** (not, opposite of):
- *unknown, unseen, uncertain, unforgettable*

### Grammar: Comparison

**Comparative** (comparing two):
- *Her fear was **greater than** his.*
- *The situation became **more frightening** as night fell.*
- *He ran **faster than** he had ever run before.*

**Superlative** (comparing three or more):
- *It was **the scariest** moment of her life.*
- *That was **the most terrifying** experience they had ever had.*

**Irregular forms**:
- *good / better / best*
- *bad / worse / worst*
- *far / farther/further / farthest/furthest*

**As ... as** (equality): *The second cave was **as dark as** the first.*
**Less ... than** (negative): *The challenge was **less difficult than** expected.*
