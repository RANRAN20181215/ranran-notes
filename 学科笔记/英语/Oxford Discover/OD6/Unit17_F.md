## Unit 17 (F) — The White Giraffe

### BQ9: Why are stories important?

**Genre**: Novel Excerpt / Adventure Fiction
**Reading Strategy**: Make Inferences

### Vocabulary

| Word | POS | Definition |
|------|-----|------------|
| orphan | n/v | a child whose parents have died |
| reserve | n | an area of protected land for wildlife |
| poacher | n | a person who hunts illegally |
| legend | n | a traditional story sometimes based on history |
| bond | n/v | a connection; to form a connection |
| courage | n | bravery in the face of danger |
| mysterious | adj | difficult to understand or explain |

**Words in Context**: Africa, wildlife, conservation, myth, bond, freedom

### Word Study
Suffix **-ion** (noun-forming from verbs):
- *protect → protection, imagine → imagination, connect → connection*

### Grammar: Word Formation

**Prefixes** change meaning:

| Prefix | Meaning | Example |
|--------|---------|---------|
| un- | not | *unforgettable, unknown* |
| re- | again | *retell, rebuild* |
| pre- | before | *preview, predict* |
| mis- | wrongly | *misunderstand, mislead* |
| over- | too much | *overcome, overprotect* |

**Suffixes** change word class:
- *care* (v) → *careful* (adj) → *carefully* (adv) → *carefulness* (n)

**Writing Focus**: Write a story (original narrative)
