## Unit 6 (NF) — Uncovering Masks

### BQ3: Why do we wear masks?

**Genre**: Informational Text (Nonfiction)
**Reading Strategy**: Main Idea and Details

### Vocabulary

| Word | POS | Definition |
|------|-----|------------|
| covering | n | something that covers the surface of something |
| disguise | n | a thing that changes your appearance |
| entertainment | n | things to amuse or interest people |
| essential | adj | extremely important |
| urgent | adj | needing immediate attention |
| lifesaver | n | something that helps in a difficult situation |
| shield | v | to protect someone or something |
| performer | n | a person who entertains an audience |
| elaborate | adj | detailed and carefully designed |
| crucial | adj | vitally important |
| symbolic | adj | representing something else |
| ritual | n | a ceremony performed in a set way |

**Words in Context**: cultural, tradition, ceremony, identity, spiritual, artisan

### Word Study
Suffix **-tion** / **-sion** (noun-forming):
- *entertainment → entertainment, perform → performance*
- *disguise → disguising, protect → protection*

### Grammar: Passive with Modals

**Form**: modal + be + past participle

| Modal | Example |
|-------|---------|
| can be + pp | *Masks can be found in every culture.* |
| must be + pp | *The costume must be finished by Friday.* |
| should be + pp | *The materials should be chosen carefully.* |
| may/might be + pp | *The design may be changed for the festival.* |

**Use**: describing what is possible, necessary, or advisable in passive voice
