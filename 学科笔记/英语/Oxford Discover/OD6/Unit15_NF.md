## Unit 15 (NF) — Fear on the Brain

### BQ8: What are we afraid of?

**Genre**: Informational Text (Nonfiction)
**Reading Strategy**: Identify Main Idea and Supporting Details

### Vocabulary

| Word | POS | Definition |
|------|-----|------------|
| fear | n | an unpleasant emotion caused by danger |
| phobia | n | an extreme or irrational fear |
| anxiety | n | a feeling of worry or unease |
| trigger | v/n | to cause something to happen; a cause |
| respond | v | to react to something |
| instinct | n | an innate, automatic behavior |
| threat | n | a sign of possible danger |
| survival | n | the state of continuing to live |
| reaction | n | a response to something |
| irrational | adj | not based on reason or logic |
| overcome | v | to succeed in dealing with a fear |
| courageous | adj | brave; not afraid to face danger |

**Words in Context**: amygdala, brain, fight-or-flight, danger, safety, adrenaline

### Word Study
Suffix **-ous** (adjective-forming):
- *courage → courageous, danger → dangerous, mystery → mysterious*

### Grammar: Linking Words

**Purpose**: connect ideas, show relationships between clauses

| Type | Words | Example |
|------|-------|---------|
| Contrast | however, although, but, yet | *Fear can protect us, **however** it can also limit us.* |
| Cause/Reason | because, since, as | **Because** our brain senses danger, we react quickly.* |
| Result | therefore, so, consequently | *The threat was real, **so** she ran.* |
| Addition | moreover, furthermore, in addition | *Fears can be learned. **Moreover**, they can be unlearned.* |
| Purpose | in order to, so that | *We study fear **in order to** understand it better.* |

**Writing Focus**: Write an instructional text (how to face/manage fear)
