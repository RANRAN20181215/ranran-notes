## Unit 10 (F) — The Whistlers

### BQ5: How do we use language?

**Genre**: Fiction (Cultural Story)
**Reading Strategy**: Draw Conclusions

### Vocabulary

| Word | POS | Definition |
|------|-----|------------|
| whistle | v/n | to make a high-pitched sound with the mouth |
| echo | v/n | a reflected sound |
| signal | n | a sign or action that communicates something |
| canyon | n | a deep valley with steep sides |
| code | n | a system of words, letters, or signals |
| transmit | v | to send out over a distance |

**Words in Context**: shepherds, mountain, valley, message, listener, practice

### Word Study
Suffix **-al** (adjective-forming):
- *origin → original, nature → natural, culture → cultural*

### Grammar: Reported Speech

**Direct speech** → **Reported speech** (tense shift):

| Direct | Reported |
|--------|----------|
| present simple → | past simple |
| present continuous → | past continuous |
| past simple → | past perfect |
| will → | would |
| can → | could |
| must → | had to |

**Say vs. Tell**:
- *He said (that) the whistles carried for miles.*
- *He told me (that) the whistles carried for miles.*

**Questions**: *She asked if/whether the villagers still used the code.*
**Commands**: *He told us to listen carefully.*
