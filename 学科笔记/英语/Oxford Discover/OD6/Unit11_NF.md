## Unit 11 (NF) — View from the Summit

### BQ6: Why do we record history?

**Genre**: Biography / Historical Account (Nonfiction)
**Reading Strategy**: Cause and Effect

### Vocabulary

| Word | POS | Definition |
|------|-----|------------|
| expedition | n | a journey undertaken for a purpose |
| summit | n | the highest point of a mountain |
| ledge | n | a narrow shelf projecting from a cliff |
| altitude | n | height above sea level |
| conquer | v | to overcome and take control of |
| endurance | n | the ability to withstand difficulty |
| triumph | n | a great victory or achievement |
| explore | v | to travel through an unknown area |
| document | v | to record in writing or other form |
| perseverance | n | continued effort despite difficulty |
| legacy | n | something handed down from the past |
| milestone | n | an important stage or event |

**Words in Context**: expedition, base camp, rope, oxygen, climbing, route

### Word Study
Suffix **-ance** / **-ence** (noun-forming):
- *endure → endurance, persevere → perseverance, exist → existence*

### Grammar: Passive Voice (all tenses)

| Tense | Form | Example |
|-------|------|---------|
| Present Simple | is/are + pp | *The summit is reached by few.* |
| Past Simple | was/were + pp | *The expedition was documented in photographs.* |
| Present Perfect | has/have been + pp | *Many peaks have been climbed.* |
| Future | will be + pp | *The story will be told for generations.* |
| Modal | can/must be + pp | *History must be recorded accurately.* |

**Key use**: when the agent is unknown, obvious, or less important than the action
