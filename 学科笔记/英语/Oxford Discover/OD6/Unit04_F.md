### Word Study
Words with **ie** and **ee**: *field, shield, piece* / *sleet, fleet, greet*

### Grammar: Past Perfect Simple

**Form**: had + past participle

**Affirmative**: *Pliny had studied philosophy before he became an admiral.*
**Negative**: *He hadn't seen a volcano erupt before that day.*
**Questions**: *Had they ever witnessed such an event?*

**Use**: an action completed before another past action; "the past in the past"
**Signal words**: already, just, never, ever, before, after, by the time
