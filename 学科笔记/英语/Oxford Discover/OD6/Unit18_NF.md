## Unit 18 (NF) — Why Stories Matter

### BQ9: Why are stories important?

**Genre**: Persuasive Essay (Nonfiction)
**Reading Strategy**: Fact vs Opinion

### Vocabulary

| Word | POS | Definition |
|------|-----|------------|
| narrative | n | a spoken or written account of events |
| moral | n | a lesson learned from a story |
| imagination | n | the ability to create images in the mind |
| empathy | n | the ability to understand others' feelings |
| character | n | a person in a story |
| plot | n | the main events of a story |
| theme | n | the central idea or message |
| perspective | n | a point of view |
| influence | v/n | the power to affect someone or something |
| tradition | n | a long-established custom or belief |
| oral | adj | spoken rather than written |
| heritage | n | cultural traditions passed down |

**Words in Context**: folktale, legend, myth, fairy tale, wisdom, generation

### Word Study
Suffix **-ity** (noun-forming from adjectives):
- *curious → curiosity, creative → creativity, possible → possibility*

### Grammar: Consolidation Review

Review of all OD6 grammar:

| BQ | Grammar | Key Example |
|----|---------|-------------|
| 1 | Future Continuous; Continuous Tenses | *People will be telling stories for centuries.* |
| 2 | Modals; Past Perfect | *Stories can teach valuable lessons.* |
| 3 | The Passive; Passive with Modals | *Stories are passed down through generations.* |
| 4 | Conditionals; Wishes | *If stories didn't exist, culture would be lost.* |
| 5 | Relative Clauses; Reported Speech | *She said that stories connect us all.* |
| 6 | Passive Review; Reflexive Pronouns | *Stories are enjoyed by people of all ages.* |
| 7 | Gerunds and Infinitives; Phrasal Verbs | *Children enjoy hearing stories before bed.* |
| 8 | Linking Words; Comparison | *A good story is **more powerful than** a lecture.* |
| 9 | Word Formation | *The **imagination** of the storyteller brings tales to life.* |

**Writing Focus**: Write an opinion piece about why stories matter
