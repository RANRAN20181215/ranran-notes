## Unit 9 (NF) — Talking About Language

### BQ5: How do we use language?

**Genre**: Informational Text (Nonfiction)
**Reading Strategy**: Summarize

### Vocabulary

| Word | POS | Definition |
|------|-----|------------|
| communicate | v | to share information or feelings |
| dialect | n | a regional form of a language |
| fluent | adj | able to speak easily and smoothly |
| gesture | n | a movement of the body to express meaning |
| interpret | v | to explain the meaning of something |
| origin | n | the beginning or source of something |
| translate | v | to change words from one language to another |
| evolve | v | to develop gradually over time |
| grammar | n | the rules of a language |
| vocabulary | n | all the words a person knows |
| tone | n | the attitude expressed in speech or writing |
| context | n | the situation in which something is said |

**Words in Context**: linguist, bilingual, slang, formal, informal, expression

### Word Study
Prefix **tele-** (at a distance):
- *telephone, television, telescope, telegram*

### Grammar: Relative Clauses

**Defining relative clauses** (necessary information):
- *A dialect is a regional form **that/which** people speak.*
- *A linguist **who** studies language can identify origins.*

**Non-defining relative clauses** (extra information, with commas):
- *English, **which** has Germanic roots, borrowed many French words.*

| Pronoun | Use |
|---------|-----|
| who | people |
| which | things, animals |
| that | people or things (defining only) |
| where | places |
| when | times |
| whose | possession |
