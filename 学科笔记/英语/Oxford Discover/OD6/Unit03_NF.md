## Unit 3 (NF) — Inside Our Planet

### BQ2: What is the Earth made of?

**Genre**: Informational Text (Nonfiction)
**Reading Strategy**: Evaluate

### Vocabulary

| Word | POS | Definition |
|------|-----|------------|
| mass | n | a large amount of matter |
| crust | n | the outermost layer of the Earth |
| sphere | n | a round, ball-shaped object |
| comprise | v | to consist of; to be made up of |
| chamber | n | an enclosed space or room |
| chunk | n | a thick, solid piece |

**Words in Context**: mantle, intense, erupt, continental, sections, enormous

### Word Study
Homonyms (words with same spelling/pronunciation but different meanings):
- *bank* (river / money), *wave* (ocean / hand), *rock* (stone / music)

### Grammar: Modals

**Common modals**:
- **can**: ability (*Geologists can tell the date of some rocks.*)
- **must**: necessity (*Scientists must study the evidence carefully.*)
- **should**: advice (*We should protect our planet.*)
- **may/might**: possibility (*There may be water beneath the surface.*)
- **could**: past ability / polite request

**Form**: modal + base verb (no -s, no to)
