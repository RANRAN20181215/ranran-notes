## Unit 5 (F) — A Season of Discontent

### BQ3: Why do we wear masks?

**Genre**: Play / Drama (Fiction)
**Reading Strategy**: Identify Characters' Feelings

### Vocabulary

| Word | POS | Definition |
|------|-----|------------|
| discontent | n | dissatisfaction; unhappiness |
| disguise | v/n | to change appearance to hide identity |
| conceal | v | to hide |
| pretend | v | to act as if something is true |
| hollow | adj | empty inside |
| mask | n/v | a covering for the face; to hide feelings |
| burden | n | a heavy load (physical or emotional) |
| confess | v | to admit to something |
| reveal | v | to show; to make known |
| genuine | adj | real; authentic |
| suppress | v | to keep hidden; to hold back |
| hypocrite | n | someone who pretends to be what they are not |
| vulnerable | adj | open to emotional harm |

**Words in Context**: discontent, facade, sincerity, disguise, mask, pretense

### Word Study
Prefix **dis-** (not, opposite of):
- *disagree, disappear, dishonest, discontent*

### Grammar: The Passive (present and past simple)

**Form**: be + past participle

| Tense | Form | Example |
|-------|------|---------|
| Present Simple | is/am/are + pp | *The mask is made of wood and paint.* |
| Past Simple | was/were + pp | *The performance was watched by hundreds.* |

**Use**: when the action is more important than the doer; when the doer is unknown or obvious
