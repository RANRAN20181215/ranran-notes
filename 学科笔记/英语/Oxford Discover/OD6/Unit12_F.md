## Unit 12 (F) — A Housemaid's Diary

### BQ6: Why do we record history?

**Genre**: Historical Fiction / Diary
**Reading Strategy**: Point of View

### Vocabulary

| Word | POS | Definition |
|------|-----|------------|
| diary | n | a daily record of personal experiences |
| servant | n | a person employed to work in a household |
| witness | v/n | to see something happen; a person who saw it |
| ordinary | adj | normal; not special |
| injustice | n | unfair treatment |
| memoir | n | a historical account written from personal knowledge |
| perspective | n | a particular way of viewing things |
| record | v/n | to set down in writing |
| preserve | v | to keep in original condition |
| generation | n | all people born around the same time |
| humble | adj | modest; not proud |
| historian | n | an expert in or writer of history |

**Words in Context**: household, master, daily life, social class, duty, account

### Word Study
Suffix **-ful** and **-less**:
- *meaningful, careful, useful*
- *meaningless, careless, useless*

### Grammar: Reflexive Pronouns

| Subject | Reflexive |
|---------|-----------|
| I | myself |
| you | yourself / yourselves |
| he | himself |
| she | herself |
| it | itself |
| we | ourselves |
| they | themselves |

**Uses**:
- The same person does & receives the action (*She taught herself to write.*)
- For emphasis (*The queen herself visited the house.*)
- With prepositions (*He kept the diary to himself.*)
- Common expressions: *by myself, enjoy yourself, behave yourself*
