### Grammar: Continuous Tenses

| Tense | Form | Example |
|-------|------|---------|
| Present Continuous | is/am/are + verb-ing | *The engineer is building a bridge.* |
| Past Continuous | was/were + verb-ing | *The engineer was building a bridge.* |
| Present Perfect Continuous | has/have been + verb-ing | *The engineer has been building a bridge.* |
| Future Continuous | will be + verb-ing | *The engineer will be building a bridge.* |

**Use**: actions in progress at a specific time
