### Grammar: Phrasal Verbs

**What are phrasal verbs?** verb + particle (preposition/adverb) that together have a special meaning.

| Phrasal Verb | Meaning | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| figure out | solve, understand | *Scientists figured out how crows use tools.* |
| pick up | learn casually | *Birds pick up new behaviors from each other.* |
| look out for | watch carefully | *They look out for predators.* |
| break down | analyze | *Let's break down the problem.* |
| carry out | perform | *The researchers carried out an experiment.* |
| point out | draw attention to | *The article points out key findings.* |

**Separable vs Inseparable**:
- Separable: *figure it out* ✓ (object between verb and particle)
- Inseparable: *look after the chicks* ✓ (object after particle)

**Writing Focus**: Write an action plan (how to help/protect birds)
