# 📘 Unit 9 Following Rules

> Big Question 5: **How do people get along with each other?**
> 阅读类型: 信息文（非虚构）| 阅读策略: Categorize

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 规则 (rules) 帮助我们和别人好好相处
- **At school**：take turns, be polite, clean up, don't litter
- **In the library**：be quiet, don't run, return books on time
- **On the road**：traffic lights, crossing guard, look both ways
- **In the community**：lifeguard at the pool, principal at school
- 规则不是限制我们，而是保护我们
- Let's categorise（分类）rules by location

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Possessive Pronouns

| 人称 | 物主代词 | 含义 | 对比: 物主形容词 |
|:----|:--------|:----|:----------------|
| I | **mine** | 我的 | my + 名词 → mine |
| you | **yours** | 你的 | your + 名词 → yours |
| he | **his** | 他的 | his + 名词 → his |
| she | **hers** | 她的 | her + 名词 → hers |
| it | **its** | 它的 | its + 名词 → its |
| we | **ours** | 我们的 | our + 名词 → ours |
| they | **theirs** | 他们的 | their + 名词 → theirs |

**例句：**

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| This book is **mine**. | 这本书是我的。 |
| That pencil is **yours**. | 那支铅笔是你的。 |
| The red rule is **hers**. | 那条红色规则是她的。 |
