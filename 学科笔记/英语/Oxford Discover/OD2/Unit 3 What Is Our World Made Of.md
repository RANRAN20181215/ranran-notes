# 📘 Unit 3 What Is Our World Made Of?

> Big Question 2: **How do things change?**
> 阅读类型: 信息文（非虚构）| 阅读策略: Cause and Effect

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 世界上的物质有三种状态：solid, liquid, gas
- **Solid**（固体）：形状固定，不流动
  - 举例：ice, rock, wood, table
- **Liquid**（液体）：会流动，形状随容器变化
  - 举例：water, milk, juice
- **Gas**（气体）：看不见，充满空间
  - 举例：steam, air
- 物质可以改变状态：加热 melt（融化），冷却 freeze（冻结）
- 水最常见：solid = ice, liquid = water, gas = steam
- 因果关系：Heat causes ice to melt. / Cold causes water to freeze.

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Simple Past of Verb To Be (was/were)

| 人称 | 肯定 | 否定 |
|:----|:----|:----|
| I | I **was** a solid. | I **was not** (wasn't) a liquid. |
| He/She/It | It **was** ice. | It **wasn't** water. |
| You/We/They | They **were** solids. | They **were not** (weren't) gases. |

---

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| How do things change? | 东西怎么变化？ |
| Ice was a solid. | 冰之前是固体。 |
| Water is a liquid. | 水是液体。 |
| The ice melted because it was hot. | 冰融化了，因为天气热。 |
| The water was in the freezer. | 水之前在冰箱里。 |
| Heat causes ice to melt. | 热使冰融化。 |

## 🎮 Fun Activities 趣味活动

3. **🔥 Cause & Effect Chain**：画出因果链 (heat → ice melts → water)
