# 📘 Unit 4 Let's Make Ice Cream!

> Big Question 2: **How do things change?**
> 阅读类型: 剧本（虚构）| 阅读策略: Cause and Effect

---

## 📖 Story 读一读

- 一个剧本 (play)：几个孩子想做冰淇淋
- 他们准备了材料：cream, sugar, salt, ice
- 把 cream 和 sugar 放进一个小袋子 (plastic bag)，封好 (closed)
- 把 ice 和 salt 放进一个大袋子
- 把小袋子放进大袋子，封好，用力摇！
- 为什么加盐？盐让冰更冷，帮助 cream 凝固
- 摇啊摇，摇到 mixture 变成了 ice cream！
- 孩子们开心地吃着自己做的冰淇淋
- 他们学到了：液体可以通过冷却变成固体

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Simple Past of Verb To Be — Questions

| 类型 | 问句 | 回答 |
|:----|:----|:----|
| Was + 单数? | **Was** the ice cold? | Yes, it **was**. / No, it **wasn't**. |
| Were + 复数? | **Were** the ingredients ready? | Yes, they **were**. / No, they **weren't**. |
| 特殊疑问句 | Why **was** the salt in the bag? | The salt **was** there to make it colder. |
| 特殊疑问句 | **Were** you happy? | Yes, I **was**. |

---

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| Was the mixture cold? | 混合物冷吗？ |
| Yes, it was. | 是的，很冷。 |
| Why was the salt there? | 为什么盐在那里？ |
| The salt was there to make the ice colder. | 盐在那里是为了让冰更冷。 |
| First, we poured the cream into the bag. | 首先，我们把奶油倒进袋子里。 |
| How do things change? | 东西怎么变化？ |
