# 📘 Unit 13 Properties of Matter

> Big Question 7: **What are natural resources made of?**
> 阅读类型: 信息文（非虚构）| 阅读策略: Using a Table of Contents

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- Everything around us is made of **matter**（物质）
- 所有物质都有 **properties**（性质）：
  - **Color**（颜色）：red, blue, yellow...
  - **Shape**（形状）：round, square, long...
  - **Size**（大小）：big, small, long, short
  - **Texture**（质地）：rough（粗糙的）/ smooth（光滑的）
  - **Weight**（重量）：heavy / light
  - **Flexibility**（柔韧性）：bendy（可弯曲的）/ stiff（硬的）
- 物质有三态：
  - **Solid**（固体）：holds its shape, hard to squeeze
  - **Liquid**（液体）：flows, takes shape of its container
  - **Gas**（气体）：fills the space, invisible

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Countable and Uncountable Nouns

| 类型 | 定义 | 例子 |
|:----|:----|:----|
| **Countable nouns** | 可数名词 | apple, pencil, bottle, chair, ball |
| **Uncountable nouns** | 不可数名词 | water, air, wood, glass, paper, plastic |

**a / an / some / any**

| 句型 | 可数单数 | 可数复数 | 不可数 |
|:----|:--------|:--------|:------|
| 肯定句 | There **is a** ball. | There **are some** balls. | There **is some** water. |
| 否定句 | There **isn't a** ball. | There **aren't any** balls. | There **isn't any** water. |
| 疑问句 | **Is** there **a** ball? | **Are** there **any** balls? | **Is** there **any** water? |

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| What are natural resources made of? | 自然资源是由什么构成的？ |
| What are the properties of matter? | 物质有什么性质？ |
| Wood is stiff. It is not bendy. | 木头是硬的，不可弯曲。 |
| Water flows. It is a liquid. | 水会流动。它是液体。 |
| There is some water in the glass. | 玻璃杯里有一些水。 |
| There aren't any solids in this bottle. | 这个瓶子里没有固体。 |
