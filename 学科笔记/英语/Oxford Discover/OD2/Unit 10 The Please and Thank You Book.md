# 📘 Unit 10 The Please and Thank You Book

> Big Question 5: **How do people get along with each other?**
> 阅读类型: 诗歌（虚构）| 阅读策略: Theme

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 一本关于礼貌的诗集
- **Knock** before you enter a room
- Say **please** when you ask for something
- Say **thank you** when you receive something
- Don't **grab** (抢), wait your turn
- **Pass** things to others, don't throw
- **Put away** your toys after playing
- **Invite** others to play with you
- **Wash** your hands before eating
- **Share** your snacks with friends
- 核心主题 (theme)：礼貌让世界更美好

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Can and May for Permission

| 句型 | 用法 | 例句 |
|:----|:----|:----|
| **Can** I + 动词? | 口语中表许可 | **Can** I go outside? |
| **May** I + 动词? | 更正式表许可 | **May** I use your pen? |
| 肯定回答 | 允许 | Yes, you **can/may**. |
| 否定回答 | 不允许 | No, you **can't/may not**. |

**对比：**

| 英文 | 语气 |
|:----|:----|
| Can I have some water? | 日常/口语 |
| May I come in? | 更礼貌/正式 |
| You may not run in the hallway. | 禁止/正式规则 |
