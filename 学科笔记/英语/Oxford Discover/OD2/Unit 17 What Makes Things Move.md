# 📘 Unit 17 What Makes Things Move

> Big Question 9: **What makes things move?**
> 阅读类型: 信息文（非虚构）| 阅读策略: 顺序 (Sequence)

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 要让东西动起来，需要 **force**（力）
- **Push**（推）：away from you
- **Pull**（拉）：toward you
- **Gravity**（重力）：pulls things down
- **Friction**（摩擦力）：slows things down
- 力的发现和应用：
  - 古人用 animals（动物）拉车
  - Wheels（轮子）让移动更容易
  - Simple machines（简单机械）帮我们省力
- Sequence（顺序）— 力的发展史：
  1. People walked everywhere
  2. People used animals to carry things
  3. People invented the wheel
  4. People made wagons and carts
  5. People invented cars, trains, and planes

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Conjunctions 连词

| 连词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|:----|:----|:----|
| **and** | 连接两个并列内容 | I push **and** pull. |
| **but** | 表示转折 | I want to go, **but** I'm tired. |
| **or** | 表示选择 | Push **or** pull? |
| **because** | 表示原因 | I'm tired **because** I ran. |
| **so** | 表示结果 | It was heavy, **so** I pulled it. |

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| What makes things move? | 什么让东西动起来？ |
| A force makes things move. | 力让东西动起来。 |
| Push the door to open it. | 推门来打开它。 |
| Pull the rope toward you. | 把绳子拉向你。 |
| Gravity pulls things down. | 重力把东西往下拉。 |
| The wheel made it easier to move things. | 轮子让搬东西更容易。 |
| I push the box, **and** it moves. | 我推箱子，它就动了。 |
| I want to go fast, **but** friction slows me down. | 我想快，但摩擦力让我慢下来。 |
