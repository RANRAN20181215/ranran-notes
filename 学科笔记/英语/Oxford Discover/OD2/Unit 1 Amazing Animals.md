# 📘 Unit 1 Amazing Animals

> Big Question 1: **How are animals different from one another?**
> 阅读类型: 信息文（非虚构）| 阅读策略: Compare and Contrast

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 动物之间有很多不同之处
- **Mammals**（哺乳动物）：有 fur/hair，用 lungs 呼吸，妈妈喂奶
  - 举例：dogs, cats, whales
- **Reptiles**（爬行动物）：有 scales，冷血，用 lungs 呼吸
  - 举例：snakes, lizards, turtles
- **Amphibians**（两栖动物）：生活在水中和陆地，有 moist skin
  - 举例：frogs, salamanders
- **Birds**（鸟类）：有 feathers 和 wings，产蛋
- **Fish**（鱼类）：有 scales 和 gills，生活在水中
- 动物通过 skin（皮肤）、body covering、breathing 方式不同来区分

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Subject and Object Pronouns

| 主格 (Subject) | 宾格 (Object) | 例句 |
|:-------------:|:-------------:|:----|
| I | me | The dog sees **me**. |
| you | you | **I** see **you**. |
| he | him | **He** has fur. **I** see **him**. |
| she | her | **She** has feathers. **I** see **her**. |
| it | it | **It** has scales. **I** see **it**. |
| we | us | **We** like animals. The cat sees **us**. |
| they | them | **They** are birds. **I** see **them**. |

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| How are animals different? | 动物之间有什么不同？ |
| Mammals have fur. | 哺乳动物有毛发。 |
| Birds have feathers. | 鸟类有羽毛。 |
| Fish have scales and gills. | 鱼有鳞片和鳃。 |
| I see them at the zoo. | 我在动物园看到它们。 |
| The frog has moist skin. | 青蛙有湿润的皮肤。 |
