# 📘 Unit 5 Then and Now

> Big Question 3: **How are things different now from long ago?**
> 阅读类型: 信息文（非虚构）| 阅读策略: Main Idea and Details

---

## 📖 Reading 读一读

- 过去和现在的生活有很多不同
- **Travel**（旅行）：过去人们步行或骑马，现在有 airplane, car, bus
- **Communication**（通讯）：过去人们写信 (letter)，现在发 e-mail 和 text message
- **News**（新闻）：过去听 radio，现在看 Internet 和 TV
- **Entertainment**：过去人们读书、听故事，现在看电影、玩游戏
- 科技让生活更方便，但有些美好的东西也变少了

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Simple Past Regular Verbs

### 规则动词过去式

| 规则 | 原形 | 过去式 |
|:----|:----|:------|
| 一般 + ed | walk / play | walked / played |
| 以 e 结尾 + d | live / use | lived / used |
| 辅音 + y → ied | study / try | studied / tried |
| 重读闭音节双写 + ed | stop / clap | stopped / clapped |

### 肯定 & 否定

| 肯定 | 否定 |
|:----|:----|
| People **walked** everywhere. | People **didn't walk** everywhere. |
| They **used** horses. | They **didn't use** cars. |
| He **listened** to the radio. | He **didn't listen** to the radio. |
| She **watched** TV. | She **didn't watch** TV. |

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| How are things different now from long ago? | 现在和过去有什么不同？ |
| People walked everywhere long ago. | 以前人们走路去任何地方。 |
| They didn't have cars. | 他们没有汽车。 |
| My grandmother listened to the radio. | 我奶奶听收音机。 |
| Now, we use the Internet. | 现在我们用互联网。 |
| Long ago, people wrote letters. | 以前人们写信。 |

## 🎧 Listening & Speaking 听说练习

- 听关于过去和现在交通方式的对话
- 口语练习：Asking for Something to Be Repeated（请求重复）
- 用 "Pardon?" / "Can you say that again?" / "I'm sorry?"

---

## ✍️ Writing 写作

- 规则动词加 -ed
- 写 4 句对比（过去 vs 现在）
- 示例：Long ago, people **walked**. Now, they **drive**. Long ago, they **listened** to the radio. Now, they **watch** TV.

## 🎮 Fun Activities 趣味活动

1. **📸 Then & Now Picture Sort**：把过去和现在的图片配对对比
2. **👴 Interview an Adult**：采访长辈，用 "Did you use...?" / "What did you do?"
3. **📊 Venn Diagram**：画韦恩图对比过去和现在
