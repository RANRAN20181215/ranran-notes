# 📘 Unit 18 The Boy Who Cried Wolf

> Big Question 9: **What makes things move?**
> 阅读类型: 寓言（虚构）| 阅读策略: Setting

---

## 📖 Story 读一读

- 一个男孩负责看管羊群 (watch the sheep)
- 他很无聊 (bored)，想找点乐子
- 他大喊 "Wolf! Wolf!"（狼来了！）
- 村民们 (villagers) 赶紧跑上来救他
- 结果根本没有狼——男孩大笑 "It was just a joke!"
- 村民们很生气地走了
- 第二天，男孩又大喊 "Wolf! Wolf!"
- 村民们又跑上来——又没有狼
- 第三天，狼真的来了！
- 男孩大喊 "Wolf! Wolf! Please help me!"
- 但是没有人来——没人相信他了
- 狼把羊都吃掉了
- **Moral**（寓意）：Don't tell lies, or no one will believe you when you tell the truth.

## 📝 Grammar 语法: Conjunctions in Past Tense

| 连词 | 过去式例句 |
|:----|:----------|
| **and** | The boy watched the sheep **and** felt bored. |
| **but** | He cried wolf, **but** there was no wolf. |
| **or** | Did he tell a joke **or** a lie? |
| **because** | No one came **because** they didn't believe him. |
| **so** | The wolf came **so** the sheep ran away. |

### General and Specific Setting

| Setting 类型 | 例子 |
|:------------|:----|
| General（大背景） | long ago, in a small village |
| Specific（具体） | on a hill, near the woods |

## 💡 Key Sentences 重点句型

| 英文 | 中文 |
|:----|:----|
| What makes things move? | 什么让东西动起来？ |
| The boy cried wolf. | 那个男孩喊狼来了。 |
| The villagers ran up the hill. | 村民们跑上山。 |
| No one believed him. | 没人相信他。 |
| Why didn't anyone come? | 为什么没有人来？ |
| Because no one believed the boy. | 因为没人相信那个男孩。 |
| The boy was bored, **so** he played a joke. | 男孩很无聊，所以他开了个玩笑。 |
