# Grammar Friends · Level 1

> 适合：一~二年级 · 写单词，不要求写完整句子

---

## 单元速览

| 单元 | 语法点 | 例句 | ⚠️ 冉冉易错 |
|:----:|:------|:-----|:----------|
| 1 | be 动词 am/is/are | I am Tom. She is Anna. | am/are 搞混 |
| 2 | a/an | a book, an apple | an 总忘 |
| 3 | 复数 -s/-es | dogs, buses, boxes | es 忘加 |
| 4 | have got | I have got a pen. She has got... | has/have 分不清 |
| 5 | 形容词 | a big dog, a red car | 顺序放错 |
| 6 | can / can't | I can jump. | 与 can't 发音搞混 |
| 7 | 现在进行时 | I am sitting. He is running. | am/is/are 漏掉 |
| 8 | 一般现在时 | I get up at 7. She gets up... | 第三人称单数加 -s |
| 9 | there is / there are | There is a cat. | is/are 分不清 |
| 10 | 介词 in/on/under | The book is on the table. | in/on 搞混 |
| 11 | 祈使句 | Sit down. Open the door. | 前面常加 to 写成 to sit |
| 12 | 疑问词 what/where | What is this? Where is it? | 语序 |

---

## ⚠️ 冉冉最可能卡的地方

### 1. 第三人称单数 (-s)
```
I like      →  He likes      ← 容易忘加 -s
They run    →  She runs
```
**方法：** 每次说 he/she/it 的时候，让他手比一个 S 的手势，提醒自己

### 2. have got / has got
```
I have got  →  She HAS got   ← 容易说成 She have got
```
**方法：** he/she/it → has，其他 → have，就两句话

### 3. 现在进行时忘 am/is/are
```
I am playing.      ← 不是 I playing
She is sleeping.   ← 不是 She sleeping
```
**方法：** 告诉他 "一个动作正在发生 → be 动词 + 动词-ing"

---

## 📝 小口诀

```
I 用 am，you 用 are，
is 跟着 he/she/it。
复数都用 are，
单数用 is 要牢记。
```
