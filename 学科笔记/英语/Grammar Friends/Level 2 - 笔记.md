# Grammar Friends · Level 2

> 适合：二~三年级 · 开始写短句

---

## 单元速览

| 单元 | 语法点 | 例句 | ⚠️ 注意 |
|:----:|:------|:-----|:-------|
| 1 | be 动词复习 | We are happy. | 人称代词巩固 |
| 2 | have got 复习 + 否定 | I haven't got a pet. | 否定式 hasn't/haven't |
| 3 | 一般现在时 + 频率词 | always/usually/sometimes/never | always 放动词前 |
| 4 | 现在进行时 | He is reading a book. | 拼写规则（双写末尾字母） |
| 5 | can/can't + 能力 | She can swim. | 后面不加 to |
| 6 | there is/are 否定+疑问 | Is there a park? | some → any |
| 7 | 过去时 be 动词 | I was happy. We were sad. | was/were 区分 |
| 8 | 一般过去时（规则动词） | I walked to school. | -ed 发音（/t/ /d/ /Id/） |
| 9 | 一般过去时（不规则） | go→went, eat→ate | 不规则动词表 |
| 10 | 比较级 | bigger, taller, more beautiful | 双写+er vs more |
| 11 | 最高级 | the biggest, the tallest | 前面加 the |
| 12 | 时间表达 | at 5 o'clock, on Monday, in July | at/on/in 介词 |

---

## ⚠️ 冉冉可能卡的地方

### 1. 过去时 -ed 发音
```
walked  /t/    →  walkt
played  /d/    →  playd
wanted  /Id/   →  wantid
```
清辅音后读 /t/，浊辅音后读 /d/，t/d 结尾读 /Id/

### 2. 频率词位置
```
I always get up at 7.  ← 正确
I get up always at 7.  ← 错误（频率词放实义动词前）
```

### 3. some → any（否定和疑问）
```
There are some books.
→ Are there ANY books?    ← 疑问句用 any
→ There aren't ANY books. ← 否定句用 any
```
